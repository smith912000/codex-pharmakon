Codex Pharmakon Universalis

Codex Pharmacologicus Universalis

A comprehensive pharmacological synthesis of the world's medicinal substances, integrating nine source traditions from the Ebers Papyrus (ca. 1550 BCE) to contemporary Level A clinical evidence. Each entry is a full 8-section synthesis: nomenclature, cross-traditional presence, convergence analysis, phytochemical composition, mechanisms of action, clinical evidence, phenomenological record, and practical application.
