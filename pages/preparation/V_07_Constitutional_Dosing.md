# V.07 — Dosing by Constitution
## *Ayurvedic Dosha · TCM Pattern · Traditional Energetic Constitutional Tier · Scaling Principles*

---

### The Constitutional Principle

No dose is universal. The same substance at the same dose produces different effects in different constitutional types — a fact recognized by every traditional pharmacological system and now being validated by the emerging science of pharmacogenomics. The traditional energetic system integrates three constitutional maps to provide the practitioner with the most nuanced dosing framework available.

---

### Ayurvedic Dosha Dosing Modifications

**Vata Constitution** (Air + Ether — thin, variable, mobile, cold, dry, light)
*Pattern:* Irregular digestion; variable appetite; quick mind; prone to anxiety, insomnia; joints crack; cold extremities

| Category | Modification |
|----------|-------------|
| Preferred substance types | Warming, heavy, oily, grounding Jing tonics |
| Avoid | Cold-property herbs, excessive bitter herbs, high-dose stimulants |
| Dose | Start at 50–75% standard dose (Vata absorbs intensely but irregularly) |
| Carrier | Always with ghee, warm milk, or warm water + pinch of salt |
| Optimal stacks | Ashwagandha + Shatavari + Ghee + Cistanche |
| Timing | Regular, consistent timing (Vata benefits from routine) |

**Pitta Constitution** (Fire + Water — medium build, sharp, intense, hot, slightly oily)
*Pattern:* Strong digestion; competitive; prone to inflammation, skin conditions, irritability, excess Yang heat

| Category | Modification |
|----------|-------------|
| Preferred substance types | Cooling, slightly bitter, Yin-nourishing, anti-inflammatory |
| Avoid | Warming Yang tonics (Ginseng, Morinda, Cistanche) in excess; alcohol tinctures |
| Dose | Standard dose; excellent metabolism; good absorption |
| Carrier | Coconut milk, cool water, aloe vera gel base |
| Optimal stacks | Shatavari + Brahmi/Bacopa + Centella + Boswellia + Pearl Powder |
| Timing | Cooling tonics in afternoon (Pitta time is 10am–2pm — avoid heating supplements) |

**Kapha Constitution** (Earth + Water — heavy, stable, cool, slow, moist)
*Pattern:* Strong build; slow but steady; prone to weight gain, congestion, lethargy; excellent endurance; poor motivation peaks

| Category | Modification |
|----------|-------------|
| Preferred substance types | Stimulating, light, warming, drying Qi-movers |
| Avoid | Heavy Yin tonics (E-Jiao, Shatavari in excess); oily preparations |
| Dose | May require 125–150% of standard dose (slower absorption, efficient storage — needs stronger signal) |
| Carrier | Honey + warm water; ginger tea; no ghee in excess |
| Optimal stacks | Rhodiola + Mucuna + Jiaogulan + Eleuthero + Black Seed |
| Timing | Morning activation emphasis; avoid heavy tonics at night |

### TCM Pattern Dosing Modifications

**Yin Deficiency (陰虛)** — dry, hot, thin, restless, red tongue without coat
- *Avoid:* All Yang-warming tonics (Ginseng, Morinda, Deer Antler at high dose, Cistanche)
- *Emphasize:* Shatavari, E-Jiao, He Shou Wu, Pearl Powder, Bacopa
- *Dose:* Lower doses of Jing tonics; emphasize Yin-nourishing over Yang-building

**Yang Deficiency (陽虛)** — cold, fatigued, pale, swollen tongue with white coat
- *Emphasize:* Eucommia, Morinda, Cistanche, Deer Antler, Ginseng (Red)
- *Avoid:* Cooling herbs (Shatavari in excess, bitter cold herbs)
- *Dose:* Full standard dose; can tolerate higher Yang-tonic doses

**Qi Deficiency (氣虛)** — fatigue, pale, breathless, weak digestion, soft pulse
- *Emphasize:* Astragalus, Eleuthero, Jiaogulan, Ginseng, Maca
- *Avoid:* Blood-moving dispersing herbs (Myrrh, Dan Shen in excess)
- *Dose:* Start at 50% dose; build slowly — Qi-deficient digestion cannot process heavy doses

**Qi Stagnation (氣滯)** — tight, bloated, frustrated, irregular, wiry pulse
- *Emphasize:* Turmeric, Boswellia, Myrrh, Tulsi, Schisandra
- *Avoid:* Heavy Jing-consolidating tonics (too consolidating when channels are blocked)
- *Dose:* Standard dose; add Qi-moving herbs before adding Jing tonics

**Blood Deficiency (血虛)** — pale lips/nails, scanty periods, poor sleep, thin
- *Emphasize:* E-Jiao, He Shou Wu, Goji, Deer Antler, Colostrum
- *Avoid:* Excessive dispersing herbs
- *Dose:* Standard; emphasize food-medicine integration

**Phlegm-Damp (痰濕)** — heavy, foggy, congested, swollen, coated tongue
- *Avoid:* Rich Yin tonics (E-Jiao, Shatavari, Ghee in excess), heavy Jing tonics
- *Emphasize:* Digestive spices (ginger, cardamom, black pepper) with everything; Jiaogulan; light Qi-movers
- *Dose:* Reduced doses; add digestive herbs to all preparations

### Traditional Energetic Constitutional Tier

The traditional energetic system defines four constitutional tiers based on the practitioner's *current Jing-Qi-Shen balance*:

**Dense Tier** — High Jing reserve, strong Yang, robust constitution; responds well to Qi and Shen work; Jing tonics should be maintained but not overly emphasized
**Meridional Tier** — Balanced constitution; standard protocols apply
**Diaphanous Tier** — Depleted Jing, refined nervous system, excellent Shen sensitivity; requires deep Jing restoration before Qi activation; sensitive to stimulating herbs
**Mixed Tier** — Jing depletion in some domains (physical) with excess in others (mental); requires targeted protocols

### Body Weight Scaling

| Body Weight | Scale Factor |
|-------------|-------------|
| < 60kg (132 lb) | 0.75x standard dose |
| 60–80kg (132–176 lb) | 1.0x standard dose |
| 80–100kg (176–220 lb) | 1.1x standard dose |
| > 100kg (220 lb) | 1.25x standard dose |

**The Starting Low Protocol:**
Any new practitioner, regardless of constitution, should begin all new substances at 25–50% of the target therapeutic dose and increase by 25% increments every 5–7 days. This allows:
- Allergy/sensitivity detection before significant exposure
- Gut flora adaptation (prevents digestive upset from rapid changes)
- Endogenous system adjustment (prevents rebound from sudden receptor saturation)
- Identification of the *minimum effective dose* — which varies dramatically between individuals
