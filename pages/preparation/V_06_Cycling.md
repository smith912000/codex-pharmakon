# V.06 — Cycling and Elimination
## *8-Week Protocol · Detox Herbs · Signs of Over-Supplementation · Clearing Concept*

---

### The Principle of Cycling

Herbal and mineral tonics are not inert chemicals — they communicate with biological systems through receptors, signaling pathways, and adaptive responses. Like training itself, consistent unvarying stimulation produces *adaptation* (tolerance) and eventually *resistance* (the same dose produces diminishing effect). Conversely, abrupt cessation after prolonged use can produce "withdrawal" of the body's own production of the compounds being supplemented.

The cycling principle addresses both: **use a substance long enough to produce its full therapeutic effect, then rest long enough for the body's own systems to reassert their baseline function**.

---

### The Standard 8-Week Kenetica Cycle

```
Weeks 1–2:   Introduction (50% of therapeutic dose — allow receptor adaptation)
Weeks 3–6:   Therapeutic dose (full protocol per Volume V_05_Stacking)
Week 7:      Taper (50% dose reduction — prevent withdrawal effects)
Week 8:      Elimination week (specific clearing herbs only — no tonics)
Rest 2–4 wk: True rest (observe return to baseline; note what returns, what is absent)
```

The *rest period observation* is pharmacologically essential — it reveals which improvements are genuine vs. which were dependent on the supplement. If all improvements disappear during the rest period, the substance was compensating for a deeper deficiency that has not been addressed.

### Elimination Week Protocol

**Detox herbs (clear accumulation without depleting):**

**Triphala (Haritaki + Bibhitaki + Amalaki)** — 2g at bedtime
- The Ayurvedic "three fruits" formula; gentle bowel clearing, liver support, antioxidant reset
- The most appropriate elimination herb for the end of tonic cycles

**Milk Thistle (Silymarin)** — 200–400mg 3x daily with meals
- Hepatoprotective and liver-regenerating; silymarin stimulates hepatocyte repair and upregulates glutathione S-transferase
- Essential after heavy mineral supplementation cycles (Shilajit, metal compounds)

**Dan Shen (Salvia miltiorrhiza 丹参)** — 3–5g decoction daily
- TCM's premier "blood clearing" herb; removes accumulated Blood-stasis from the fascial-vascular system
- Used specifically after intensive training cycles where microtrauma accumulation has occurred

**Chlorella** — 3–5g daily with water
- Binds heavy metals and fat-soluble toxins in the gut; absorbs metabolites released from fat tissue during weight loss or intense training
- Start with 1g/day and increase gradually (die-off/binding symptoms possible)

**Activated charcoal** — 1g, 2 hours away from all supplements
- Acute binding agent for elimination weeks; non-selective adsorber

**Nettle leaf tea** — 3 cups daily
- Diuretic; mineral-dense; promotes lymphatic clearance; kidney tonic

### Signs of Over-Supplementation (Depletion from Excess)

| System | Signs of Depletion | Likely Cause |
|--------|---------------------|-------------|
| Adrenal | Morning fatigue despite sleep, afternoon crash, low stress tolerance | Overstimulating adaptogens without rest cycles |
| Liver | Headaches, chemical sensitivities, right upper quadrant tightness | Accumulated Bhasma, tannin, or alkaloid load |
| Kidney | Lower back achiness, increased urination, reduced libido despite tonic use | Yang-driving without Yin balance; mineral overload |
| Nervous system | Jitteriness, insomnia, heat sensations, restlessness | Stimulating Qi-movers (Rhodiola, Ginseng, Mucuna) without Shen-stabilizers |
| Digestive | Bloating, loose stools, loss of appetite | Rich Jing tonics (Cistanche, E-Jiao) without digestive support |
| General | The herb "stops working" | Receptor downregulation from continuous use; cycle needed |

### The Clearing Concept

The traditional pharmacological synthesis on cycles uses the metaphor of the *river channel*:

*"A river that only receives and never clears its bed becomes silted and stagnant. The strongest river is one that periodically floods — running fast enough to clear what has accumulated. After the flood, the channel runs clear and its current is strong again."*

The elimination week is the deliberate flood. It is not a punishment or a failure — it is *pharmacological wisdom*. The practitioner who resists the elimination week out of fear of losing their gains has misunderstood the nature of Jing cultivation: the substance is only the instruction. The body must have space to follow it.
