### The Three-Pillar Stack Concept

The TCM/Ayurvedic stacking framework is organized around three functional pillars that must be balanced in every protocol:

**Pillar 1 — ◈ Jing Base (Structural Foundation)**
One or more Jing-restorative substances that rebuild the constitutional substrate. Without this pillar, Qi activators drain the practitioner further.

**Pillar 2 — ◉ Qi Activator (Circulatory Drive)**
One or more Qi-moving or Qi-building substances that ensure the Jing being built is actually circulated and utilized. Without this pillar, Jing tonics become static and create "stagnant richness."

**Pillar 3 — ☿/⊕ Shen-Opener or Yuan-Sealer (Regulatory Control)**
One substance that either opens the Shen channels (for skill-refinement phases) or seals the Yuan constitution (for deep recovery phases). Without this pillar, the Jing and Qi work lacks direction.

### Contraindicated Combinations

| Combination | Risk | Reason |
|-------------|------|--------|
| Ginseng + Stimulants (caffeine, ephedra) | Overstimulation, hypertension | Additive stimulant loading |
| Mucuna + MAOIs | Hypertensive crisis | L-DOPA + MAO inhibition → dopamine excess |
| He Shou Wu (raw) + Any | Liver toxicity | Raw form hepatotoxic; processed form only |
| Multiple blood-thinners (Turmeric + Nattokinase + Fish Oil + Garlic + Aspirin) | Bleeding risk | Additive anticoagulant effect |
| Warming Yang tonics (Morinda, Cistanche, Ginseng) in Yin-deficient heat | Constitutional aggravation | Feeding fire in a dry field |
| Valerian + Prescription benzodiazepines | Excessive CNS depression | Additive GABA-A enhancement |
