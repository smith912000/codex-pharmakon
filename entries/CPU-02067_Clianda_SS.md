### Origin & Tradition

> **IDENTITY CAUTION.** The plant is not identified, and even the spelling is uncertain. This entry is a historical record only. Nothing in it is a recommendation.

**Named only by the Sushruta Samhita among the texts on disk, under this spelling.** This is a single-text entry with exactly one attestation. The row began on 13 October as an unverified placeholder taken from the group list in chapter 38; this entry replaces it. The names.csv row gives "no identification given here". A search of all the Sushruta text on disk (the chapter files and the three Bhishagratna volumes) for "Clianda" found one place only.

**Sushruta Samhita (SS).** *Sutrasthana* ch.38, the Eladi group (`Sources/SS_Sushruta/text/Bhishagratna_Vol1_djvu.txt` line 15509, the names.csv row says 15508; `text/sutra/SS_Sutra_ch38.txt` line 168). The passage reads:

> "The Elardi Group: - The group of medicinal drugs known as the Eladi-Gana consists of Ela, Tagara, Kushtha, Mansi, Dhyamaka, Tvaka, Patra, Naga-pushpa, Priyangu, Harenuka, Vyaghranakha, Shukti, Clianda, Sthauneyaka, Shriveshtaka, Chocha, Choraka, Valaka, Guggulu, Sarjarasa, Turushka, Kunduruka, Aguru, Sprikka, Ushira, Bhadradaru, Kumkuma, Punnaga and Keshara."

(The heading "Elardi" in the e-text is an OCR slip for Eladi.) The metrical text gives the virtue of the whole group (Vol. I lines 15512 to 15520): "The therapeutic virtue of the group consists in subduing the action of Vayu and Kapham and in neutralising the effects of poison. It is a cosmetic and arrests the eruption of pimples and other vegetations on the skin such as rash, urticaria etc. and checks the itching sensation incidental thereto." This is the group's virtue, not a statement about this member alone.

The drug is not named in any recipe in the text on disk, and the Bhishagratna glossary in Vol. III was searched for it without a hit. Nothing is said of its part, taste or form. The spelling should be checked against the printed page of the 1907 translation, where the name may be different. [USER: verify]
