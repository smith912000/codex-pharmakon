# Chinese black cicada — 柞蝉 *zuochan* (older 蚱蝉)

## *A large singing cicada of the fifth month; Cryptotympana atrata (Fabricius) is the modern identification for the Chinese drug, which is ours and of low confidence: the classic and its commentary describe the insect only by its call, its season and its tree, and the genus is not resolved* | *(Volume III — Animal)*

**Tradition Tags:** SBJ

---

### Origin & Tradition

**Named only by the *Shennong bencao jing* among the texts on disk.** The entry rests on one text in one edition, Sun Xingyan's 1799 reconstruction (`Sources/SBJ_Shennong/text/Shennong_SunXingyan_1799_daizhige.txt`). The existing entry III_328 (*Cicada*, tag CM) is a different record: Avicenna's *ṭāṭīqus*, identified there as *Cicada orni*, from a different tradition. The two are not equated, and nothing is merged.

**Divine Farmer's Classic (SBJ).** The name line is 柞蝉 (line 2689). The classic's own paragraph is at line 2691: 味咸，寒。主小儿惊痫、夜啼，[a character lost]病，寒热。生杨柳上。 In English: "Flavour salty, cold. Governs in children fright-epilepsy (惊痫) and night crying (夜啼), [lost] illness, cold and heat. Grows on poplars and willows (生杨柳上)." The paragraph has no 久服 clause and names no part. The classic places the entry in the middle class (中经; heading 卷二 中经 at line 2697). The preface (line 1677) describes the middle class as nourishing the nature, non-toxic or toxic, chosen as fit.

**Mingyi bielu (《名医》, later additions) layer.** Line 2693: "gathered in the fifth month; steamed and dried" (五月采，蒸干之). The whole insect is the Codex's reading of this line; no part is named.

**Sun Xingyan's note (案).** Line 2695 is a long philological note whose subject is how the name is to be written. Sun says the old text read 蚱蝉, and quotes the *Bielu*: "蚱 is the singing cicada; the husk is also called [a name lost] 蝉, also 伏[ ]". He argues that 蚱 is the same as 柞: the *Zhou li* "Kaogong ji" has "if broad, 柞", and Zheng Xuan reads 柞 as the 咋 of 咋咋然, "a loud sound outward"; the *Shuowen* says "诸 is a great sound, in sound the same as 柞". On this ground "today I follow 柞" (今据作柞). The 柞蝉 is then "the 蜩 that sings in the fifth month" (即五月鸣蜩之蜩), citing the *Xia xiaozheng* ("in the fifth month the good 蜩 sings", with the commentary "the good 蜩, having five colours"), the *Erya* (蜩, 螂蜩), the *Mao shi* ("like a 蜩"; "蜩 is 蝉"), and the *Fangyan*, which gives regional names for the cicada (Chu: 蜩; between Song and Wei: 螗蜩; between Chen and Zheng: 螂蜩; Qin and Jin: 蝉; Haidai: a name lost). The *Lunheng* is cited for the life cycle, "the cicada is born from the 复育 and comes out by splitting its back" (蝉，生于复育，开背而出). The *Yupian* says "蚱蝉 is born in the seventh month", which is a different season from the fifth. Tao Hongjing read the character 蚱 as 笮 and took it to be the "cold cicada" (寒蝉) of the *Yueling* and the [lost] of the *Erya*, and Sun notes that the "Tang edition" commentary rejects this. In short, this note shows that the old scholars disagreed about which cicada the name meant, and about its season (fifth or seventh month). The Codex has no basis to settle it.

### Active Components

None is stated in the text. As a general fact, an insect's body is chiefly protein, with a chitinous cuticle, and the cast husk of a cicada is chitin. The Codex has no source on the chemistry of the material the classic meant and names no compound. [USER: verify]

### Effect & Use Case in Kenetica

Historical record only, and not a recommended practice. The classic records a salty, cold insect for children's fright-epilepsy, night crying, and cold and heat, with no preparation beyond the Mingyi layer's steaming and drying. The Codex gives no protocol and does not translate "fright-epilepsy" into a modern diagnosis. A child with seizures or with persistent crying needs prompt medical assessment.

### Preparation & Dosing

The only preparation the sources give is the Mingyi layer's gathering in the fifth month and "steamed and dried". No part, quantity, frequency or duration is given. No dose is given in the sources retrieved. [USER: verify]

### Scientific Correlation

### Cultivation Register

**Divine Farmer's Classic (SBJ).** *(b) Framed.* The entry stands in the middle class (中经), which the preface describes as nourishing the nature and sometimes toxic. Its 味 paragraph has no 久服 clause and no life-nourishing promise (*Shennong bencao jing*, Sun Xingyan ed. 1799, middle classic, entry 柞蝉 *zuochan*, `Sources/SBJ_Shennong/text/Shennong_SunXingyan_1799_daizhige.txt` line 2691). Its status is therapeutic only. The Mingyi and 案 layers add none.

**Practitioner's note.** There is no cultivation practice here. The entry is a short middle-class record of a cicada for children's night crying and fright, with a long commentary about how to write its name, and no dose.

### Contraindications

The classic does not warn. No safety information for children, allergy or other medicines was read, and none is asserted. Insect material may carry contaminants. Seizures and persistent crying in a child need prompt medical assessment. No conservation information was read. [USER: verify]
