# Pinus longifolia — "Pinus Longifolia" and "Resin of Pinus longifolia" (Bhishagratna, Sushruta Samhita, Appendix No. 3)

## *Pinus roxburghii Sarg. (syn. P. longifolia Roxb.), the chir pine, a long-needled pine of the Himalayan foothills whose resin is the usual source of Indian turpentine and rosin; our identification, from the Latin name as printed and the standard synonymy, with two appendix lines as the only basis; confidence medium, because the appendix gives no description* | *(Volume I — Plant-Based)*

### Origin & Tradition

**Named by the Sushruta translation only, in its Appendix 3 name list.** This is a single-text entry. The row began on 13 October as an unverified placeholder; it is now sourced to two lines of the same appendix.

**Sushruta (SS).** Two lines of Appendix No. 3 of Bhishagratna's translation, headed "Names of Drugs (with Latin synonyms)" (`Sources/SS_Sushruta/text/Bhishagratna_Vol3_djvu.txt`, heading at lines 31119-31121). The translator's note at the end of the appendices (lines 33351-33354) says he drew on *Vaidyaka-Sabdasindhu*, Wilson's *Sanskrit and English Dictionary*, *Indian Plants and Drugs* and *Kavirsiji S'iksha*. The Latin names are therefore the translator's glosses from those works, not words of the Samhita itself.

The first line (32847) prints "Pinus  Longifolia." in a run that includes "Saccbarum  Sara." before it and the cross-reference `See  "Trivoit."` after it (the e-text's spelling; the translator's cross-reference form is as printed and its relation to the pine line is not clear). The second line (32991) prints "Resin  of  Pinus  longifolia." between "Gmelina  arborea.  Aegle  Marmellos." and "Gum  of  Boswellia  Thuripera.". That second gloss shows that the drug the translator names there is the resin of the tree, not the tree itself. This is the only place in the sources read that names a part.

In this e-text the Sanskrit-name column and the Latin-name column have been separated into different blocks. The extract rows (`Sources/SS_Sushruta/extract/names.csv`, lines 32847 and 32991) record both as "Appendix 3 Latin column; not paired to a Sanskrit name (OCR column split)".
