# Silverfish — 衣鱼 *yiyu* ("clothes fish"), also 白鱼 ("white fish")

## *The silverfish, a wingless insect found in clothes and books; Lepisma saccharina is the modern identification, which is ours and of medium confidence (Ctenolepisma species are alternatives). The classic's own words describe only the animal's habitat and name* | *(Volume III — Animal)*

**Tradition Tags:** SBJ

---

### Origin & Tradition

**Named only by the *Shennong bencao jing* among the texts on disk.** The entry rests on one text in one edition, Sun Xingyan's 1799 reconstruction (`Sources/SBJ_Shennong/text/Shennong_SunXingyan_1799_daizhige.txt`). A search of the other texts on disk for *Lepisma*, silverfish and *Ctenolepisma* found nothing.

**Divine Farmer's Classic (SBJ).** The name line is 衣鱼 (line 4021). The classic's own paragraph is at line 4023: 味咸，温，无毒。主妇人疝瘕，小便不利（《御览》作泄利），小儿中风（《御览》作头风）、项强（《御览》作疆），背起摩之。一名白鱼。生平泽。 In English: "Flavour salty, warm, non-toxic (无毒). Governs in women *shan* and *jia* (疝瘕, two classical terms for lower-abdominal pain and masses that the Codex keeps untranslated), difficult urination (小便不利; the *Yulan* has 'diarrhoea' 泄利), and in children wind-strike (中风; the *Yulan* has 'head wind' 头风) with stiff neck (项强; the *Yulan* writes the second character differently). 背起摩之: literally 'where the back rises, rub it'. Also called 白鱼. Grows in marshes and flat wet ground." The last instruction is given literally, and the Codex does not interpret it. [USER: verify the rendering of 背起摩之] The paragraph has no 久服 clause. The paragraph states "non-toxic" for a lower-class drug, a point on which it differs from the preface's general description of that class. The Codex records the difference and does not resolve it.

The classic places the entry in the lower class (下经; the group tally at line 4031 reads 上虫、鱼，下品一十八种。旧同, "insects and fishes, lower class, eighteen kinds; same as before", and the section heading 卷三 下经 follows). The preface (line 2935) describes the lower class as for treating disease, mostly toxic, not to be taken long.

**Wu Pu (《吴普》, third century) layer.** Line 4025: "the white fish in clothes (衣中白鱼); also called [a character lost]" (quoted from the *Yulan*).

**Mingyi bielu (《名医》, later additions) layer.** Line 4027: "also called [a character lost]; grows at Xianyang" (生咸阳).

**Sun Xingyan's note (案).** Line 4029: the *Shuowen* says "[ ] is 白鱼"; the *Guangya*: "白鱼 is [ ]鱼"; the *Erya*: "[ ] is 白鱼"; and Guo Pu: "an insect in clothes and in books, also called 蚋鱼" (衣、书中虫，一名蚋鱼). Guo Pu's gloss is the clearest statement of the animal: an insect that lives in clothes and books. The rest of the note is lost characters.

### Active Components

None is stated in the text. As a general fact, the silverfish is a small primitive insect (order Zygentoma) whose body is covered in silvery scales. The Codex has no source on its chemistry and names no compound. [USER: verify]

### Effect & Use Case in Kenetica

Historical record only, and not a recommended practice. The classic records the insect for women's lower-abdominal complaints, difficult urination, and, in children, neck stiffness after "wind-strike", with a rubbing instruction. The Codex gives no protocol and does not turn *shan*, *jia* or "wind-strike" into modern diagnoses. Neck stiffness in a child, and difficulty in passing urine, need prompt medical assessment. The animal is a household pest, and no collection or use is recommended.

### Preparation & Dosing

The classic gives one instruction, 背起摩之 ("rub it where the back rises"), which suggests an external use, but the Codex does not say how the insect was prepared. No part, quantity, frequency or duration is given. No dose is given in the sources retrieved. [USER: verify]

### Cultivation Register

**Divine Farmer's Classic (SBJ).** *(b) Framed.* The entry stands in the lower class (下经), which the preface describes as for treating disease and not for long use. Its 味 paragraph has no 久服 clause and no life-nourishing promise (*Shennong bencao jing*, Sun Xingyan ed. 1799, lower classic, entry 衣鱼 *yiyu*, `Sources/SBJ_Shennong/text/Shennong_SunXingyan_1799_daizhige.txt` line 4023). Its status is therapeutic only. The Wu Pu, Mingyi and 案 layers add none.

**Practitioner's note.** There is no cultivation practice here. The entry is a short lower-class record of a household insect used for women's and children's complaints, with a single rubbing instruction and no dose.

### Contraindications

The classic's paragraph calls the material non-toxic, but the Codex read no source that confirms this, and no safety information for pregnancy, children or other medicines was read. A household insect may carry dirt or moulds. Pain, masses, urinary difficulty and a stiff neck in a child need medical assessment. No conservation information was read. [USER: verify]
