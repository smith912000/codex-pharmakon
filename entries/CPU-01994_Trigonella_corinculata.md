# Trigonella corinculata — Prikka (Sprikka) (Sushruta, Bhishagratna's Appendix 3 and Sutra ch. 38)

## *Trigonella corniculata (L.) L., a small leguminous herb of the fenugreek genus; the printed "corinculata" is a misspelling of corniculata; the Sanskrit name Prikka is paired with it by position in a damaged two-column list and is probably the Sprikka of the Eladi group, so the identification is the translator's by position: species high confidence, Sanskrit pairing medium* | *(Volume I — Plant-Based)*

**Tradition Tags:** SS

---

### Origin & Tradition

**Single-text entry.** The only attestation is the Sushruta Samhita in Bhishagratna's English translation. A different row of the Codex, Fenugreek (*Trigonella foenum-graecum*, CPU-00136), covers the better-known member of the genus and is not this row.

**Sushruta (SS).** The Latin name stands once in "Appendix No. 3. Names of Drugs (with Latin synonyms)" (`Sources/SS_Sushruta/text/Bhishagratna_Vol3_djvu.txt`, line 32587): "...  Trigonella  Corinculata." The Sanskrit names of the P section run from *Pippali* (line 32521) to *Pundarika*, and the Latin list runs from "Piper longum" (line 32579) to "Nelumbium speciosum". The Latin synonyms before and after this line fall against Sanskrit names in a consistent order: "Piper longum" against *Pippali*, "Paederia foetida" against *Prasarani*, "Hemionites cordifolia" against *Prisniparni*, "Aglaia Roxburghiana" against *Priyangu* (line 32541, as OCR prints it), "Areca Catechu" against *Puga*, "Boerhavia diffusa" against *Punarnava*, "Nelumbium speciosum" against *Pundarika*. That leaves the Sanskrit name between *Prasarani* and *Prisniparni*, which the OCR prints as *Prikka* (line 32537). The pairing is by position. [USER: verify against the printed page.]

In the body of the translation, Sutrasthana ch. 38 (the classification of drugs into groups) lists in the Eladi group the drugs "Ela, Tagara, Kushtha, Mansi, Dhyamaka, Tvaka, Patra, Nagapushpa, Priyangu, Harenuka, Vyaghranakha, Shukti, Chanda, Sthauneyaka, Shriveshtaka, Chocha, Choraka, Valaka, Guggulu, Sarjarasa, Turushka, Kunduruka, Aguru, Sprikka, Ushira, Bhadradaru, Kumkuma, Punnaga and Keshara" (`Sources/SS_Sushruta/text/sutra/SS_Sutra_ch38.txt` line 170; Vol. I line 15511). The metrical text that follows says the group subdues Vayu and Kapham, neutralises the effects of poison, is a cosmetic, and "arrests the eruption of pimples and other vegetations on the skin such as rash, urticaria etc." and checks itching. Whether *Sprikka* is the Appendix's *Prikka* is our inference from the near-identical spelling; the translation does not say. [USER: verify.] The group's action is stated for the group as a whole, not for any one drug in it.

No separate recipe of this herb was found in the chapter files. The text does not state a part used, an effect or a preparation for it alone.

### Active Components

No source on disk names a constituent. In general terms, *Trigonella* belongs to the pea family (Fabaceae) and fenugreek-type seeds and leaves contain mucilage and aromatic compounds. Nothing is stated for *T. corniculata* in the sources read. [USER: verify any named constituent before adding it.]

### Effect & Use Case in Kenetica

**Historical record only. Not a practice.** If the Eladi-group reading is right, the herb is one of twenty-nine aromatic drugs taken together for a group effect on Vayu, Kapham, poison and skin eruptions. The text on disk does not isolate it. The Codex gives no protocol and does not transfer uses from fenugreek.

### Preparation & Dosing

The sources retrieved give no preparation and no dose for this herb. The Eladi group is named without quantities. No dose is given in the sources retrieved. [USER: verify.]

### Scientific Correlation

General, well-established facts only. *Trigonella corniculata* is an annual legume related to fenugreek, with a curved pod. No clinical studies were retrieved for this entry. [USER: verify]

### Traditional Energetic Classification

- **SS.** No rasa, virya or vipaka is printed for this name in the lines read. The only qualities stated are those of the Eladi group as a whole (above), and only if the identification of Sprikka holds.

### Cultivation Register

**Sushruta (SS).** *(b) Framed.* If the Sprikka reading is right, the drug sits in an Eladi group whose stated action is against Vayu, Kapham, poison and skin eruptions. That is a therapeutic frame. No *rasayana* or *vajikarana* chapter read names it, so no cultivation status is attested, and none is claimed.

**Practitioner's note.** Nothing to do. A group of aromatic drugs for skin and Kapham, of which this herb may be one, is not a cultivation practice, and the identification is not secure.

### Contraindications

- **Identity.** The Sanskrit pairing and the link to the Eladi group are both inferred. Do not use a plant on the strength of this entry.
- **Safety.** The sources read say nothing. Fenugreek-type seeds are generally regarded as unsuitable in pregnancy. [USER: verify]
- **Skin conditions, poisoning.** These need proper care.
