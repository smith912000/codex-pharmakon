**Ayurvedic — Sushruta (SS).** *What the chapters say.* A search of all chapter files (`sutra`, `chikitsa`, `uttara`, `kalpa`, `nidana`, `sharira`) for Aditya-vaktra and its OCR variants found nothing. The other name, Surya-varta, is not printed as a plant anywhere in the chapters. The word "Suryavarta" does occur, but only as the name of a disease: the heading "Symptoms of Suryavarta Siroroga" (`SS_Uttara_ch25.txt` line 83, a disease of the head with pain "just at sunrise") and the treatment heading "Suryavarta and Ardhavabhedaka" (`SS_Uttara_ch26.txt` lines 171 to 213; the OCR splits the second word across two lines). A disease name and a plant name that share a spelling are two different things in the text, and nothing read links them, so those passages are not used for this plant.

*A name that begins the same way.* The chapters print Aditya-parni (`SS_Chikitsa_ch15.txt` line 303, in a list of drugs from which medicated oils may be prepared) and Adityaparnini (`SS_Chikitsa_ch30.txt` lines 29 and 217). In Chikitsa 30, a rasayana chapter, Adityaparnini is one of eighteen named "all-healing" plants, and the habitat passage says "The Adityaparnini species may be had in those two regions at the end of winter". That is a different Sanskrit name from Aditya-vaktra. The appendix lines read print no Latin for it, so nothing in the Codex ties it to *Cleome viscosa*. It is mentioned here only so that no reader treats it as this plant's attestation.

**Overlap.** A search of the entries folder for *Cleome viscosa* and *Aditya-vaktra* found no entry on this plant. I_398 (Tarkari, *Cleome gynandra*) and I_972 (*Sesbania grandiflora*) mention *Gynandropsis* or *Aditya* in other connections; I_1373 (*Aristolochia indica*) counts the same first appendix page. The Latin "Gynandropsis pentaphylla" printed on the same line as *Cleome viscosa* is also printed alone at line 31332, where by counting it falls against Arka-pushpi, not Aditya-vaktra. A separate spine row, CPU-01829 *Cleome felina* (Suvarnakshiri, line 33045), is not yet written. Nothing is merged.
