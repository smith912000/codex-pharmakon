### Scientific Correlation

Bat guano deposits can harbour the fungus *Histoplasma capsulatum*, which causes histoplasmosis when spores are inhaled, and bats are reservoirs of several zoonotic viruses. No clinical studies were retrieved for this entry. [USER: verify]

### Contraindications

- **Infection:** dry guano dust can cause histoplasmosis and other respiratory infections; it should not be handled without protection, applied to skin or taken by mouth.
- **Zoonosis:** bats can carry rabies and other viruses; contact with bats and their roosts is a hazard.
- **Conservation and law:** many bats and their roosts are legally protected, and entering or disturbing roosts may be restricted; the status was not retrieved. [USER: verify]
