The row began on 13 October as an unverified placeholder taken from the group list in chapter 38; this entry replaces it. The names.csv guess was "unresolved (unknown)"; that stands. A search of all the Sushruta text on disk (the chapter files under `Sources/SS_Sushruta/text/` and Bhishagratna's three volumes) for the name found only the group list below.

**Sushruta Samhita (SS).** Chapter 38 of the *Sutrasthana* is the chapter "which deals with the general classification of drugs according to their therapeutical properties (Dravya-Sangrahaniya-madhyaryam)" and says that "these drugs are usually made into thirty-seven different groups (Gana)" (Vol. I lines 15343 to 15350; `text/sutra/SS_Sutra_ch38.txt` lines 3 to 9). The drug is placed there by group, and a group's virtue is stated for the group as a whole.

1. **Group list.** *Sutrasthana* ch.38, the Rodhrardi group (`Sources/SS_Sushruta/text/Bhishagratna_Vol1_djvu.txt` lines 15430 to 15435; `text/sutra/SS_Sutra_ch38.txt` line 92). Bhishagratna prints: "The group of medicinal drugs known as the Rodhradi consists of Rodhra, Savararodhra, Palasha Kutannata, Ashoka, Phanji, Katphala, Elabaluka, Sallaki, Jingini, Kadamva, Sala and Kadali." It is the eighth name if "Palasha Kutannata" is counted as two names, and the seventh if as one; the print does not settle which.
2. **The group's virtue.** The metrical text gives the virtue of the group as a whole: "antidotal to the deranged Kapham and fat, is astringent in its properties, removes vaginal and uterine disorders, neutralises the effects of poison (anti toxic) and acts as a styptic and purifying agent in a case of ulcer and arrests all secretions and excretions of the body" (lines 15436 to 15441). This is the group's virtue, not a statement about Elabaluka alone.

The group list is the only place found. No description of the plant, no part used, and no rasa or potency was found, and the glossary of Bhishagratna's Vol. III did not return the name. The name begins with *Ela*, which also opens the name of the Eladi group; the text read does not say whether the two are connected, and no inference is drawn. [USER: verify against the printed page]

### Cultivation Register

**Sushruta Samhita (SS).** *(b) Framed.* The name is attested in a chapter 38 group list, which classifies drugs by the use of the whole group (*Dravya-Sangrahaniya*). Chapter 38 is not a *rasayana* or *vajikarana* chapter, so the text assigns this drug no cultivation status; it carries a therapeutic one only, and only at the level of its group. The concepts of *ojas*, *rasayana* and *vajikarana* are not claimed for it. The Rodhrardi group's virtue is astringent and wound-closing, not life-nourishing.

**Practitioner's note.** An unidentified name in a group list. Nothing supports cultivation use, and without an identification nothing can be gathered or used. A cultivator reads it as a name in a record.
