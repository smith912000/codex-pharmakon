### Origin & Tradition

Ē Jiāo is a **gelatin** — the dried, purified collagen extracted by prolonged boiling of donkey hide, formed into hard, glossy amber-black cakes. Named for Ē County in Shandong (whose particular well-water was traditionally held essential to authentic production), it has been for over two thousand years the pre-eminent **Blood-nourishing tonic** of Chinese medicine, a tribute article to the imperial court and one of the "three treasures" of Chinese tonic tradition (with ginseng and deer antler velvet). Where deer antler (I_340) builds Yang and Essence, Ē Jiāo builds **Blood and Yin** — the two are the great Yang and Yin poles of the animal-tonic materia medica.

**Chinese Tradition (Ē Jiāo):**
Ē Jiāo is sweet and neutral, entering the Lung, Liver, and Kidney. Its actions: it **tonifies the Blood** (the premier remedy for Blood-deficiency — pallor, dizziness, palpitations, and anaemia, classically for the deficiency of women after childbirth or heavy menstruation); it **nourishes Yin and moistens Dryness** (dry cough, dry throat, insomnia and irritability from Yin deficiency — a key herb in formulas for Yin-deficient wind and restlessness); it **stops bleeding** (deficiency-type uterine bleeding, blood in stool/urine, threatened miscarriage — it both nourishes and holds the Blood); and it **calms a restless fetus** and supports pregnancy. It is the archetypal gentle, deeply nourishing, moistening tonic.

**Egyptian / other traditions (EP):** animal glues and gelatins have ancient nutritive and medicinal use across traditions; the collagen-rich hide preparation as a strengthening, blood-building food-medicine is a recurring theme.

**Doctrinal & Symbolic Layer — the amber gel of nourished Blood:**
Ē Jiāo's doctrine is **nourishment and consolidation** — the rich, sticky, moistening gelatin that "fills" the empty Blood and "holds" what leaks away. Its very substance is its signature: a dense, glossy, amber concentrate of collagen, read as concentrated nourishing essence that rebuilds the Blood and moistens the dry. The strong cultural association with **women's health** — postpartum recovery, menstrual regulation, the "beauty from within" of nourished Blood — reflects the tradition's understanding of Blood as the substrate of both vitality and (in Chinese thought) the female constitution. The Codex reads Ē Jiāo as the **Yin/Blood-building pole** of the animal tonics, the moistening, consolidating complement to antler's warming, generative Yang — together the two halves of deep constitutional rebuilding. (Modern reality check: the surge in Ē Jiāo demand has created a serious global donkey-population and welfare problem — a genuine ethical dimension the Codex does not omit.)

### Active Components

- **Collagen-derived gelatin, peptides and amino acids** — Ē Jiāo is essentially purified hydrolysed collagen: rich in glycine, proline, hydroxyproline and other amino acids, plus a spectrum of bioactive peptides generated in processing. These underlie the documented haematopoietic, immunomodulatory, and tissue-supportive activities.
- **Trace minerals (iron, calcium, etc.)** — the mineral fraction contributes to the anti-anaemia/blood-building traditional reputation (iron being directly relevant to haematopoiesis).
- **(Processing note)** — authenticity and adulteration are longstanding issues (substitution with other-animal hides); genuine donkey-hide origin is a real quality-control concern.

### Scientific Correlation

*Attribution: findings below are from a review retrieved from PubMed; DOI linked.*

A review of Colla corii asini (Zhang et al., 2025, *Animal Models and Experimental Medicine*) summarises its documented biological activities — **haematopoiesis, lung-nourishing, immune enhancement, anti-infection, anti-ageing, anti-tumour and anti-fatigue effects**, with modulation of oxidative stress, immune balance and inflammation — and explores its potential (via multi-target/multi-pathway mechanisms) in chronic obstructive pulmonary disease, noting a favourable safety profile ([DOI](https://doi.org/10.1002/ame2.70077)). The haematopoietic/blood-building activity is the modern correlate of the two-thousand-year "tonify the Blood" indication; the collagen-peptide and trace-iron content provide a plausible substrate.

*Evidence-grade note: the pharmacology is largely preclinical/mechanistic; rigorous large human RCTs of Ē Jiāo as a single agent are limited (it is used in nourishing formulas and as a food-medicine). The traditional Blood/Yin-tonic use is well-established; modern data are consistent but not clinically definitive. Authenticity and donkey-welfare/sustainability are real, non-trivial concerns.*

### Contraindications

- **Weak digestion / Spleen deficiency with damp; poor appetite; loose stool:** the rich, cloying, sticky gelatin burdens weak digestion — use cautiously or with digestive herbs; avoid in damp-encumbered states.
- **Acute illness / fever / phlegm-damp:** not during acute febrile or damp-phlegm conditions.
- **Pregnancy:** classically used *to support* pregnancy (calm the fetus), but should be used under supervision.
- **Authenticity/adulteration:** genuine donkey-hide origin is a real QC issue (hide substitution is common).
- **Ethics/sustainability — significant:** soaring Ē Jiāo demand has driven a global donkey-population decline and serious welfare problems; this is a genuine ethical contraindication for indiscriminate sourcing.
- **Evidence honesty:** single-agent human RCT evidence is limited; pharmacology is preclinical; use is traditional/food-medicine.
