### Origin & Tradition

**Named only by the *Sushruta Samhita* (SS) among the texts on disk.** Single-text entry. The row's name, *Kshudrashveta Mahashveta*, is two Sanskrit words that Bhishagratna's translation prints one after the other with no comma. The first would mean "small white" and the second "great white" (a reading of the word elements, not a gloss found in the text). Whether the source means one drug, or two drugs run together by the printing, is not stated. The row began on 13 October as an unverified placeholder with the locus Sutra 38, Vol. I line 15449; this entry replaces it. No Latin name is printed.

**Ayurveda (SS).**

*The gana (Sutra ch. 38, `Sources/SS_Sushruta/text/sutra/SS_Sutra_ch38.txt` lines 102 to 111; Bhishagratna Vol. I line 15449 to 15451).* "The Arkardi Group:--The drugs known as the Arka, Alarka, the two kinds of Karanja, Nagadanti, Mayuraka, Bhargi, Rasna, Indrapushpi, Kshudrashveta Mahashveta, Vrishchikali, Alavana and Tapasha-Vriksha, enter into the composition of the group known as the Arkadi Gana." The metrical text: "The group known as the Arkadi destroys Kaphani [sic], fat, and the effects of poison. It acts as a vermifuge and a specific aseptic agent in the case of an ulcer and proves curative in diseases of the skin."

A search of the other chapter files on disk for *Kshudrashveta* and *Mahashveta* found no further occurrence. The names stand in this one group list only.

**What the sources do not give.** No description, no Latin name, no part used, and no statement whether one plant or two is meant. The group's tone (poison, worms, ulcer, skin) is the group's, not the drug's. [USER: verify]

### Cultivation Register

**Ayurveda, Sushruta Samhita (SS).** *(b) Framed.* Kshudrashveta Mahashveta is named in the Sushruta Samhita as a member of a therapeutic group (gana) and in the other places listed under Origin & Tradition. The gana is a grouping by therapeutic action. It is not a *rasayana* or *vajikarana* status, and the text read assigns Kshudrashveta Mahashveta neither. A search of the whole chapter set on disk (sutra, chikitsa, kalpa, uttara, nidana, sharira) for the name found no occurrence in the rasayana or vajikarana chapters. It occurs nowhere else in the chapters searched. Nothing links it to *ojas*, *tejas* or *prana*. The status is unattested, and only a therapeutic one is recorded.

**Practitioner's note.** One line in a group list. A cultivator has nothing to use from it, and the name should not be taken for a known plant.
