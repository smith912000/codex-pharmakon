# Shuka'a — شكاعى (*shukāʿā*, as printed; also called 'many-knotted', كثير العقد)

## *An unidentified plant with a very bitter root like *saʿd*, also called 'many-knotted'; no modern species is assigned here (confidence low)* | *(Volume I — Plant-Based)*

**Tradition Tags:** CM

---

### Origin & Tradition

**Single-text entry.** Named only by Avicenna's *Canon* among the texts on disk. Source file: `Sources/CM_Avicenna/text/IbnSina_Qanun_OpenITI_Shamela0010706.txt`.

**Locus (CM).** *Canon* Book II, entry *shukāʿā* (شكاعى), heading at Arabic text line 18203; entry to line 18210; the next heading is *shīrkhisht* (line 18211).

**What it is (CM).** 'A plant with a root like *saʿd* (cyperus-type root), very bitter, and it is also called 'many-knotted'' (هو نبات له أصل شبيه بالسعد شديد المرارة وقد يسمى كثير العقد; lines 18204-18205). **Identity doubt:** no species is stated; the cheap first pass matched this wording only. [USER: verify]

**What the text says (CM).** Its astringency is greater than that of *bādhāward*, 'especially in its bark and root' (line 18205; the entry *bādhāward* is at line 10843 and is not read here). Head: its decoction used as a mouthwash helps toothache; it and its root help swelling of the uvula. Digestive: it benefits the stomach and liver. Excretory: a decoction of its root stops women's bleeding, and as a pessary and sitz bath it helps swellings of the anus. Fevers: useful for chronic fevers, especially in children (lines 18205-18210).

### Active Components

The text names no constituent. In general terms, intensely bitter roots often carry bitter principles; this is not known for this plant.

### Effect & Use Case in Kenetica

Historical record only. The entry records a gargle, a stomach and liver remedy, an anti-bleeding decoction and a remedy for old fevers in children. It is not a recommended practice and the plant is not identified.

### Preparation & Dosing

Forms named: a mouth rinse (decoction), a decoction of the root, a pessary and a sitz bath. No dose is given in the sources retrieved. [USER: verify]
