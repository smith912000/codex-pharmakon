# Delphinium peregrinum — no Dioscorides chapter; named only on two illustration captions (spine locus 3-86)

## *Delphinium peregrinum, a binomial printed by the Dioscorides translators only in two figure captions ("Delphinium peregrinum after FAGUET — 1894") and in their index; no text of Dioscorides is assigned to it by a SUGGESTED line* | *(Volume I — Plant-Based)*

### Origin & Tradition

**Named only by the Dioscorides translation (DMM), and only in captions.** No other text read for this entry names the plant. The row was added from the extraction (ATTESTATION_QUEUE §PLANTGAP) at "3-86 (line 7294)", and the confidence there was recorded as "clear". On reading the source, that locus is not a chapter on this plant. Line 7294 is the heading *3-86. PURETHRON*, and the SUGGESTED line under it (line 7298) reads "Pyrethrum [Fuchs], Anthemis pyrethrum [Linnaeus], Anacyclus pyrethrum [in Sprague] — Pellitory of Spain, Alexander's Foot", with "other usage" names *Pyrethrum tanacetum* and *Pyrethrum balsamita*. The chapter describes a herb with a long finger-thick root "extremely burning and hot to one who tastes it", which "draws out phlegm", relieves toothache boiled in vinegar as a mouthwash, and, rubbed on with oil, "produces sweats". That is a different plant, and none of it is attributed to *Delphinium peregrinum* here. The row's locus and the identification are therefore not supported by the chapter it cites. [USER: verify]

**What the text does print.** The translation is `Sources/DMM_DioscoRides/text/Dioscorides_De_Materia_Medica_2000_English.txt`. The binomial occurs as a figure caption, "Delphinium peregrinum after FAGUET — 1894", at line 7296, directly after the 3-86 heading and following the text of 3-85 *DELPHINION ETERON*; and again at line 7989, among the figures near 3-176 *CHAMAIPITUS ETERA* (page 540). The translators' index lists *Delphinium peregrinum* at pages 463 and 540 (the index entry is at line 11697). A caption is a modern drawing chosen by the translators to illustrate a nearby chapter. It does not give a Dioscoridean description, a use or a name. The nearest plant text is 3-85 (the sibling entry I_1065, *Delphinium elatum*), which says only that "the other delphinium" is more slender than that of 3-84 and has "the same properties", less effectively. Whether the caption is the translators' illustration of 3-85 is not stated. [USER: verify]

**Overlap (chapter-level grep).** No existing entry cites 3-86; the grep for "3-86" in the entries folder matches only a number in the science text of I_757 (*Viola tricolor*), not a Dioscorides chapter. The sibling entries on neighbouring chapters are I_1066 (3-84) and I_1065 (3-85).
