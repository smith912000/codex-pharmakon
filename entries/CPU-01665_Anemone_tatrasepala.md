# Anemone tatrasepala — a scientific name printed in the Men-Tsee-Khang 2011 glossary (no Tibetan name paired)

## *Anemone tatrasepala, as the scientific name printed alone, in the scientific-name column of the Glossary on Medicinal Ingredients of the Men-Tsee-Khang 2011 text; no phonetic, Tibetan or English name is printed beside it* | *(Volume I — Plant-Based)*
**Tradition Tags:** FMT

### Origin & Tradition

**Tibetan — rGyud-bzhi (FMT).** **Named only by the Tibetan text in this row (the Men-Tsee-Khang 2011 glossary).** No other text read for this entry names the plant, and Clark 1995 does not print the binomial (searched for "tatrasepala": no match; Clark's only *Anemone* is *Anemone rivularis* at srub.ka, lines 769, 1118 and 1217, which is I_1228). The Codex does not state the botanical identity as certain. The row was added from the printed name (ATTESTATION_QUEUE §PLANTGAP) and has not been verified against POWO. [USER: verify]

*Men-Tsee-Khang 2011, Glossary on Medicinal Ingredients (line 5844).* `Sources/FMT_Tibetan/text/MenTseeKhang_2011_Root_and_Explanatory_Tantra.txt`. The glossary's first page prints the column headings "Phonetic", "Transliteration" and "Scientific Name" (lines 5051 to 5055), and a "Common Name" column appears on a later page of the same block (line 5148). On the page that begins at line 5772 the columns are printed one after another and not side by side, and the columns do not hold the same number of entries. The scientific-name column there reads, in order, from line 5798: "Glycyrrhiza glabr" (so printed), "Cinnamomum zeylannicum", "Rumex nepalensis", "Rhus vemiciflua", "-", "Acorus calamus L.", "Juniperus communis", "Trigonella foenum", "Sesbania sp.", "Capsella bursa-pastoris", "Sophora hookeriana", "Rhodiola crenulata", "Pegaeophyton sacapiflorum", "Abelmochus manihot", then (line 5844) "Anemone tatrasepala", "Androsace" (5846), "Phaeonychium parryoides" (5848), "-" (5850) and "Elettaria cardamomum" (5852). English names are interleaved in the same stretch ("Licorice", "Cinnamon", "Field sorrel", "Sweet Flag", "Juniper", "Fenugreek", "Inkberry, sesban", "Yellow soroseris", "Himalayan stonecrop", "Musk Okra"), and none is printed for the line "Anemone tatrasepala".

The phonetic names printed on the same page (lines 5774 to 5785) run from "Shingngar" to "Sumchutik", and the Tibetan transliterations from "shing mngar" to "sum cu tig" (lines 5787 to 5796). The names are not printed on one row with the scientific names, and the two columns do not hold the same number of entries ("-" stands in some places). The Codex therefore does not state which phonetic or Tibetan name goes with "Anemone tatrasepala", and it does not offer one. The extraction note on `names.csv` (row 366) says the same: "Tibetan name not paired (OCR column split)". [USER: verify]

*Searches for use.* The Men-Tsee-Khang text was searched for "tatrasepala" and "Anemone". The only hit is line 5844. The binomial does not appear in the text of the Explanatory Tantra chapter 20 (the chapter headed "The Efficacy of Medicinal Substances", line 225 of the table of contents) or in the compounding chapter, and it is not in the index. The text therefore prints no taste, power, action, part or use for it. The entry can say that the glossary lists it, and nothing else.

**Cross-reference (labelled, not a merge).** Clark prints a different *Anemone* under srub.ka (line 1118; I_1228): "halts necrosis, increases digestive heat, drains lymph". The Codex does not say that srub.ka and *Anemone tatrasepala* are the same plant, or the same drug, and nothing read ties the glossary's name to Clark's.

**Overlap.** A search of the entries folder for *tatrasepala* found no entry. I_1228 (*Anemone rivularis*) and I_1053 (*Clematis montana*) are Clark-based Tibetan entries on neighbours of srub.ka; I_1003 (*Anemone alba*, Dioscorides) and I_779 (Pasque Flower) are other *Anemone*/*Pulsatilla* entries. None is an entry on this binomial.
