# Bush darbandi — بوش دربندي (*būsh darbandī*, as printed)

## *A substance 'brought from Armenia', found in the hooves of sheep; the printed word is *shāf* (شاف), whose sense is not clear; no identification is made here (confidence low)* | *(Volume III — Animal)*

**Tradition Tags:** CM

---

### Origin & Tradition

**Single-text entry.** Named only by Avicenna's *Canon* among the texts on disk. Source file: `Sources/CM_Avicenna/text/IbnSina_Qanun_OpenITI_Shamela0010706.txt`.

**Locus (CM).** *Canon* Book II, entry *būsh darbandī* (بوش دربندي), heading at Arabic text line 11496; entry to line 11498 (the next heading is *baṭm*, which refers to the entry *al-ḥabba al-khaḍrāʾ*).

**What it is (CM).** 'It is *shāf*, brought from Armenia, found in the hooves of sheep' (هو شاف يجلب من أرمينية يوجد في أظلاف الضأن; lines 11497-11498). **Identity doubt:** the meaning of *shāf* is not established here (the word may be damaged in the OpenITI text), and *darbandī* looks like a place name (Derbent) but is not explained. The Codex has Sheep (III_176) as a general entry. [USER: verify]

**What the text says (CM).** Swellings and pustules: it is used on hot swellings and hot pustules. Joints: it is beneficial for hot gout (النقرس الحار; lines 11498-11499).

### Functional–Anatomical Correlate

The Canon maps this substance onto hot swellings, hot pustules and the joints, specifically hot gout. The text says it is used on hot swellings and hot pustules, and that it is beneficial for hot gout in the joints. The passage gives no temperament, so the only qualities in it are the heat of the conditions treated. The substance is said to be brought from Armenia and to be found in sheep's hooves, and the word shāf that names it is unclear in the text and may be damaged. No internal organ, humour, faculty or subtle-body status is named. The darbandī element looks like a place name but is not explained. The Codex names no constituent and no analytical source, and it states that the substance is not identified. Only the external use on the skin and the joints is reported, with no dose, method or preparation beyond being used on the affected place.

### Contraindications

Source-animal hygiene cautions apply (do not apply animal material to broken skin). The text states no contraindication. [USER: verify]
