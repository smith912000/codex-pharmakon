# Tripe / paunch — karsh (كرش)

## *The stomach (paunch, tripe) of an animal eaten as a food; the Canon does not say of which animal, so this is "paunch" in general (karsh being the Arabic for the stomach of a ruminant, which is the modern gloss and is [USER: verify]); confidence medium on the substance, low on the animal* | *(Volume III — Animal)*
**Tradition Tags:** CM

### Origin & Tradition

**Named only by Avicenna's *Canon* among the texts on disk.** This is a single-text entry. Book II, entry *karsh*, Arabic text from line 14311 of `Sources/CM_Avicenna/text/IbnSina_Qanun_OpenITI_Shamela0010706.txt`. A search of the other texts on disk was not run beyond the spine check. 

**Unani / Avicenna (CM).** Properties (*al-khawāṣṣ*): "little in nourishment, bad in chyme (*kaymūs*); and so are the like of it among the viscera; though their digestion be good, they are more nourishing than the lung; but the bellies of birds, when digested, are the best food, especially hens and geese" (قليل الغذاء رديء الكيموس ... لكن بطون الطير إذا انهضمت كانت أفضل غذاء وخصوصا الدجاج والأوز; the copyist's word for hens reads *al-dajālij* in the file). Digestive organs: "slow to digest" (بطيء الانهضام). The entry is a foodstuff note, with no remedy in it.

**Neighbouring line.** The line just before the heading (14310, "difficult to digest, fatty, slow to descend") belongs to the preceding entry, not to this one.

### Active Components

None is given in the text. In general, tripe is the muscular stomach wall of an animal and is mostly connective tissue and protein. This is a general fact, not from the text.

### Effect & Use Case in Kenetica

**Historical record only.** The entry is a dietary assessment: the paunch is poor food. It is not a drug and the Codex offers no use.

### Traditional Energetic Classification

Avicenna gives no heat or dryness for it; only the qualities of the food: little nourishment, bad chyme, slow digestion.

### Cultivation Register

**Unani / Avicenna (CM).** No cultivation framing is attested. **(c) Absent.** The *Canon* entry is organised by organ and complaint and says nothing here about innate heat, radical moisture or the *ruh*. The entry is food-quality only.

**Practitioner's note.** Nothing to practise. It records an ordinary food as poor food.

### Contraindications

- Hygiene: tripe is a high-risk raw material for bacterial contamination if not properly cleaned and cooked.
