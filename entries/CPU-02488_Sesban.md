# Sesban — سيبسبان (*sībasbān*, as printed)

## *Sesban: a plant of the Canon's alphabetical drug list, of which the text gives only actions; the identification is open (see doubt below)* | *(Volume I — Plant-Based)*

**Tradition Tags:** CM

---

### Origin & Tradition

**Single-text entry, and a very short one.** Named only by Avicenna's *Canon* among the texts on disk. The locus is *Canon* Book II, entry *sībasbān* (سيبسبان), Arabic text line 16150 in `Sources/CM_Avicenna/text/IbnSina_Qanun_OpenITI_Shamela0010706.txt`; the whole entry is lines 16151-16153.

**What the text says (CM).** Temperament: "like the moderate" (كالمعتدل; line 16151). Chest: "it softens the chest and the throat" (يلين الصدر والحلق). Digestive organs: "it quenches thirst, especially with its seed" (يسكن العطش وخصوصا مع بزره; lines 16152). Excretory: "it softens the belly" (يلين البطن; line 16153). There is no description (no *māhiyya* line), no choice or grade, no dose and no preparation.

**Identity doubt (high).** The English name "Sesban" is a transliteration of the Arabic heading and is the spine's. It could be taken for *Sesbania* (the genus already on the spine as I_972, *Sesbania grandiflora*), but the text gives no description, and its actions (softening chest and throat, quenching thirst, loosening the belly) would also suit the sebesten fruit (*Cordia*), whose Arabic name the heading may be a form of. That second reading is from memory and was not checked against any text on disk. [USER: verify] No identification is adopted; the entry records the name and the four actions only.
