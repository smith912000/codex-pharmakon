### Origin & Tradition

**Named by the Sushruta translation only, in its Appendix 3 name list.** This is a single-text entry. The row began on 13 October as an unverified placeholder, "Phyllanthus distichus [SS]". It is now sourced to one line of the appendix. A search of every text on disk for the Latin name found no other place that prints it.

**Sushruta (SS).** The line is `Sources/SS_Sushruta/text/Bhishagratna_Vol3_djvu.txt` line 32176, which prints ".  Phyllanthus  distichus." It sits in Appendix No. 3 of Bhishagratna's translation, headed "Names of Drugs (with Latin synonyms)" (heading at lines 31119-31121). The translator's note at the end of the appendices (lines 33351-33354) says he drew on *Vaidyaka-Sabdasindhu*, Wilson's *Sanskrit and English Dictionary*, *Indian Plants and Drugs* and *Kavirsiji S'iksha*. The Latin name is therefore the translator's gloss from those reference works. It is not a word of the Sushruta Samhita itself.

In this e-text the Sanskrit-name column and the Latin-name column of the appendix have been separated into different blocks.
