**Sushruta (SS).** In Appendix 3 of the third volume of Bhishagratna's translation (`Sources/SS_Sushruta/text/Bhishagratna_Vol3_djvu.txt`) the Sanskrit names stand in one column and the translator's identifications in a parallel column. "Asma-bheda" is at line 31283, and "Kalanchoe Laciniata." is at line 31335 in the second column.

How the pair was checked. The two columns do not sit on the same lines in the e-text, so the match was made by counting. Reading the names in order, "Arjuna" corresponds to "Terminalia Arjuna", "Arka" to "Calotropis gigantia", "Arka-mula" to "Aristolochia Indica", "Arushkara" to "See Bhallataka", "Asana" to "Terminalia Tomentosa", "Asma-bheda" to "Kalanchoe Laciniata", and "Asoka" to "Saraca Indica". Each neighbouring pair is a familiar equation, which supports the alignment, and "Asma-bheda" falls on Kalanchoe laciniata at a steady offset from them. This is a consistency check on the e-text and is not a botanical authority.
