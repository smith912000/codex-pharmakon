### Origin & Tradition

**Named only by the *Sushruta Samhita* (SS) among the texts on disk.** This is a single-text entry. The name is printed *Tapasha-Vriksha* in Bhishagratna's translation. The row began on 13 October as an unverified placeholder with the locus "SS Sutra 38, Arkardi group (Vol. I line 15450)"; this entry replaces it. A search of all the chapter files and of the three Bhishagratna volumes (including Appendix 3, the Latin synonym list) found the name once, in this list, and found no gloss.

**Ayurveda (SS).**

*The Arkadi group (Sutra ch. 38, `Sources/SS_Sushruta/text/sutra/SS_Sutra_ch38.txt` lines 102 to 117; Vol. I lines 15448 to 15452).* The group is introduced as "The Arkardi Group: — The drugs known as the Arka, Alarka, the two kinds of Karanja, Nagadanti, Mayuraka, Bhargi, Rasna, Indrapushpi, Kshudrashveta, Mahashveta, Vrishchikali, Alavana and Tapasha-Vriksha, enter into the composition of the group known as the Arkadi Gana." The metrical text (lines 113 to 117, with a page break in the middle of the list on disk): "The group known as the Arkadi destroys Kaphani, fat, and the effects of poison. It acts as a vermifuge and a specific aseptic agent in the case of an ulcer and proves curative in diseases of the skin." These are properties of the group and are not assigned to Tapasha-Vriksha singly. The translation separates the last name from the others with a hyphen, as "Tapasha-Vriksha", and gives no note; whether *vriksha* ("tree") is part of a plant name or a descriptor was not settled by any passage read. ### Cultivation Register

**Ayurveda, Sushruta Samhita (SS).** *(b) Framed.* Tapasha-Vriksha is named in the Sushruta Samhita as a member of a therapeutic group (*gana*) (the Arkadi group, Sutra ch. 38) and nowhere else. A *gana* is a grouping by therapeutic action. The group is a classification by action; it is not a *rasayana* or *vajikarana* status, and the text read assigns Tapasha-Vriksha neither. The chapters of the *Chikitsasthana* on *rasayana* and *vajikarana* (ch. 26 to 31 on disk) were searched for the name and it does not occur in them. Nothing links it to *ojas*, *tejas* or *prana*. The status is unattested; only a therapeutic one is recorded.

**Practitioner's note.** A name in one group list. A cultivator has nothing to use from it until it is identified.
