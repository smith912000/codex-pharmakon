### Active Components

Natural asphalt (bitumen) is a very viscous mixture of high-molecular-weight hydrocarbons (asphaltenes and the oily maltenes) with sulfur, nitrogen and traces of metals such as vanadium and nickel. Petroleum is the lighter liquid mixture of the same family. Both contain polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons, some of them carcinogenic.

### Scientific Correlation

Bitumen and petroleum are not safe to swallow; ingestion of petroleum products causes vomiting and aspiration pneumonitis, and prolonged skin contact with asphalt and tar products is associated with skin cancer. Asphalt is used today for roads and waterproofing. The traditional anthelmintic and skin uses have no established basis. No clinical studies were retrieved for the traditional uses. [USER: verify]

### Contraindications

Do not swallow asphalt, bitumen or petroleum: they cause vomiting and can be inhaled into the lungs, and the tars carry carcinogenic compounds; skin contact can cause burns, photosensitivity and, with long exposure, cancer. Preparations sold as "mumia" are unstandardised, and if any is made from human remains, the ethical and legal rules on human remains apply. Quarrying and sale of natural bitumen are regulated in many places. [USER: verify]
