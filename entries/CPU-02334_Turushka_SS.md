### Origin & Tradition

**Named only by the *Sushruta Samhita* (SS) among the texts on disk.** This is a single-text entry. The name is printed *Turushka* in Bhishagratna's translation. The row began on 13 October as an unverified placeholder with the locus "SS Sutra 38, Elardi group (Vol. I line 15510)"; this entry replaces it. A search of all the chapter files and of the three Bhishagratna volumes found the name once, in this list. Bhishagratna's Appendix 3 does not list it. The first-pass check suggested a duplicate of Sallaki (*Boswellia serrata*, CPU-00344); the audit disagreed, and nothing read here shows that the two are one, so the row stands on its own.

**Ayurveda (SS).**

*The Eladi group (Sutra ch. 38, `Sources/SS_Sushruta/text/sutra/SS_Sutra_ch38.txt` lines 164 to 191; Vol. I line 15510).* "The Elardi Group: — The group of medicinal drugs known as the Eladi-Gana consists of Ela, Tagara, Kushtha, Mansi, Dhyamaka, Tvaka, Patra, Naga-pushpa, Priyangu, Harenuka, Vyaghranakha, Shukti, Chanda, Sthauneyaka, Shriveshtaka, Chocha, Choraka, Valaka, Guggulu, Sarjarasa, Turushka, Kunduruka, Aguru, Sprikka, Ushira, Bhadradaru, Kumkuma, Punnaga and Keshara." The metrical text (the lines break across a page): "The therapeutic virtue of the group consists in subduing the action of Vayu and Kapham and in neutralising the effects of poison. It is a cosmetic and arrests the eruption of pimples and other vegetations on the skin such as rash, urticaria etc. and checks the itching sensation incidental thereto." These are group properties and are not assigned to Turushka singly. The group is also reckoned by Dallana in the "heat-making group" (*Ushna-varga*), according to Bhishagratna's footnote in *Uttaratantra* ch. 39 (`Sources/SS_Sushruta/text/uttara/SS_Uttara_ch39.txt` lines 1534 to 1535).

**What the sources do not give.** No Latin name, no description, no part used. The list puts it directly after Sarjarasa and Guggulu (resins) and before Kunduruka and Aguru (a resin or aromatic, and a fragrant wood), and the same neighbourhood is reported in the Kunduruka entry (I_1707). In later Sanskrit usage *turushka* is reported to name an incense resin, olibanum or a storax-type resin, but this was not found in any text opened for this entry, and the identity with *Boswellia serrata* is uncertain. The resin idea is a lead only. [USER: verify]

### Cultivation Register

**Ayurveda, Sushruta Samhita (SS).** *(b) Framed.* Turushka is named in the Sushruta Samhita as a member of a therapeutic group (*gana*) (the Eladi group, Sutra ch. 38) and nowhere else. A *gana* is a grouping by therapeutic action. The group is a classification by action; it is not a *rasayana* or *vajikarana* status, and the text read assigns Turushka neither. The chapters of the *Chikitsasthana* on *rasayana* and *vajikarana* (ch. 26 to 31 on disk) were searched for the name and it does not occur in them. Nothing links it to *ojas*, *tejas* or *prana*. The status is unattested; only a therapeutic one is recorded.

**Practitioner's note.** A name in one group list among resins and fragrant woods. A cultivator has nothing to use from it until it is identified.
