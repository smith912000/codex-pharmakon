### Active Components

No text read names a constituent. In general terms, growing antler is cartilage-like tissue that later ossifies, rich in collagen and other proteins, with minerals such as calcium and phosphate, and it is covered in skin and fine hair (the velvet). It also carries growth factors and sex hormones in small amounts, as growing tissue does. Nothing links any constituent to the actions printed in the unverified line above.

### Contraindications

- **Hormone-sensitive conditions:** velvet products can contain sex hormones; people with hormone-sensitive cancers or conditions should avoid them.
- **Quality and contamination:** velvet preparations vary and have been adulterated; no preparation is recommended.
- **Bleeding and seizures:** abnormal bleeding and epilepsy need diagnosis; the classic's actions are historical statements and not treatments.
- **Animal welfare and law:** velvet is cut from living stags, often under anaesthetic or restraint; trade in some deer parts is regulated.
- **Identity:** the deer species is not named in the lines read.
