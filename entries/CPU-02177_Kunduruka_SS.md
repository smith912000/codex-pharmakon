### Origin & Tradition

**Named only by the *Sushruta Samhita* (SS) among the texts on disk.** Single-text entry. The name is printed *Kunduruka* in Bhishagratna's translation (Vol. I). The row began on 13 October as an unverified placeholder with the locus Sutra 38, Vol. I line 15510; this entry replaces it. A search of the other chapter files on disk found no further occurrence, so this list is the only passage read. No Latin name is printed.

**Ayurveda (SS).**

*The gana (Sutra ch. 38, `Sources/SS_Sushruta/text/sutra/SS_Sutra_ch38.txt` lines 165 to 178; Bhishagratna Vol. I line 15510).* "The Elardi Group: — The group of medicinal drugs known as the Eladi-Gana consists of Ela, Tagara, Kushtha, Mansi, Dhyamaka, Tvaka, Patra, Naga-pushpa, Priyangu, Harenuka, Vyaghranakha, Shukti, Chanda, Sthauneyaka, Shriveshtaka, Chocha, Choraka, Valaka, Guggulu, Sarjarasa, Turushka, Kunduruka, Aguru, Sprikka, Ushira, Bhadradaru, Kumkuma, Punnaga and Keshara." The metrical text breaks across a page in the text on disk: "The therapeutic virtue of the group consists in subduing the action of Vayu and" (page break) "Kapham and in neutralising the effects of poison. It is a cosmetic and arrests the eruption of pimples and other vegetations on the skin such as rash, urticaria etc. and checks the itching sensation incidental thereto."

**What the sources do not give.** No Latin name and no description. The neighbours in the list include Guggulu, Sarjarasa and Turushka and Aguru, which are the resins and fragrant woods, and so the word may name a resin or aromatic. A later Sanskrit usage is reported to use *kunduru* for a frankincense-type resin, but that was not found in any text opened for this entry. The idea is only a lead. [USER: verify]

### Cultivation Register

**Ayurveda, Sushruta Samhita (SS).** *(b) Framed.* Kunduruka is named in the Sushruta Samhita as a member of a therapeutic group (gana) and in the other places listed under Origin & Tradition. The gana is a grouping by therapeutic action. It is not a *rasayana* or *vajikarana* status, and the text read assigns Kunduruka neither. A search of the whole chapter set on disk (sutra, chikitsa, kalpa, uttara, nidana, sharira) for the name found no occurrence in the rasayana or vajikarana chapters. It is named only in the Eladi group. Nothing links it to *ojas*, *tejas* or *prana*. The status is unattested, and only a therapeutic one is recorded.

**Practitioner's note.** A name in one group list. A cultivator has nothing to use from it until it is identified.
