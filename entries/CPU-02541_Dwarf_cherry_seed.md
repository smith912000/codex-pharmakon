# Dwarf cherry seed — 郁李仁 *yuliren*, also 爵李, 雀李, 车下李, 棣

## *The kernel (仁) of a small-fruited Prunus; Prunus japonica is the modern identification, which is ours and of medium confidence. P. humilis and P. pedunculata are alternatives in the modern literature, and the text's own description does not choose between them* | *(Volume I — Plant-Based)*

**Tradition Tags:** SBJ

---

### Origin & Tradition

**Named only by the *Shennong bencao jing* among the texts on disk.** The entry rests on one text in one edition, Sun Xingyan's 1799 reconstruction (`Sources/SBJ_Shennong/text/Shennong_SunXingyan_1799_daizhige.txt`). A search of the other texts on disk for *Prunus japonica*, *Cerasus japonica* and *Prunus humilis* found nothing. The existing entry I_173 (olive) names 郁李仁 once, in its "TCM Analogy" line (line 95), and does not treat the drug.

**Divine Farmer's Classic (SBJ).** The name line is 郁李仁 (line 3651). The classic's own paragraph is at line 3653: 味酸，平。主大腹水肿，面目四肢浮肿，利小便水道。根：主齿龈肿，龋齿，一名爵李。 In English: "Flavour sour, level. Governs great-belly water swelling (大腹水肿), puffy swelling of face, eyes and four limbs (面目四肢浮肿), and frees the urine and the water passages (利小便水道). The root: governs swollen gums and decayed teeth (齿龈肿，龋齿). Also called 爵李." The next line (3655) reads 生坚齿川谷, "grows ... 坚齿 ... river valleys". The words 坚齿 ("firm the teeth") sit where a habitat would be, and look displaced from the root's indications; the Codex records the printed line and builds nothing on it. [USER: verify the line] The paragraph has no 久服 clause. It names two parts: the 仁 (kernel) in the entry name, and the root.

The classic places the entry in the lower class (下经; heading 卷三 下经 at line 3707). The preface (line 2935) describes the lower class as "left-hand envoy", "governing disease to correspond to earth", "mostly toxic, not to be taken long" (主治病以应地。多毒，不可久服), for those who want to remove cold and heat and evil qi, break accumulations and gatherings and cure illness.

**Wu Pu (《吴普》, third century) layer.** Line 3657: "郁李, also called 雀李, 车下李 and 棣" (quoted from the *Yulan*).

**Mingyi bielu (《名医》, later additions) layer.** Line 3659: "also called 车下李 and 棣; grows on high mountains and hills; the root is gathered in the fifth and sixth months" (生高山及邱陵上。五月、六月采根).

**Sun Xingyan's note (案).** Line 3661 collects glosses for 棣 and 郁, the names in the aliases. The *Shuowen*: "棣 is white 棣". The *Guangya*: "山李; 雀其郁" (the transcription is corrupt). The *Erya*: "常棣, 棣"; Guo Pu: "today in Guanxi there is a 棣 tree, its fruit like a cherry (樱桃), edible". The *Mao shi* (Odes): "in the sixth month they eat 郁", with the commentary "郁 is of the 棣 kind". Liu Zhen's *Maoshi yiwen*: "the 常棣 tree is five or six *chi* high; its fruit is as big as a plum, bright red, sweet to eat". Lu Ji, in the same note: "奥李, also 雀李; found in mountains everywhere; the flower white or red; ripe and large in the sixth month; the fruit like a plum, edible". Shen Kuo's *Bu bitan* is cited for an inscription from the Jin palace ("华十四株，[ ]李一侏"). These glosses describe a small tree with red, sweet, edible, plum-like or cherry-like fruit that ripens in the sixth month, and the Codex notes that the identification with *Prunus japonica* is the modern one; the glosses are late and do not name a species. The fruit as food is the 案's subject. The classic's drug is the kernel and the root.

### Active Components

None is stated in the text. As a general fact, kernels of *Prunus* species generally contain cyanogenic glycosides (amygdalin is the best known) and fatty oil. The Codex has not retrieved a source specific to this species, and makes no claim about its content. [USER: verify]

### Effect & Use Case in Kenetica

Historical record only. The classic records a sour, level kernel for dropsical belly and limb swelling, with a promoting action on the urine, and a root for the gums and teeth. The Codex gives no protocol. The classic grades the drug in the lower class, "mostly toxic, not for long use"; the 味 paragraph itself does not call this drug toxic. Swelling of the face, eyes and limbs and dropsy need medical assessment.

### Preparation & Dosing

The classic names no preparation and no quantity. The Mingyi layer gives the gathering of the root (the fifth and sixth months). No dose is given in the sources retrieved. [USER: verify]

### Cultivation Register

**Divine Farmer's Classic (SBJ).** *(b) Framed.* The entry stands in the lower class (下经), which the preface describes as for treating disease, mostly toxic, and not for long use. Its 味 paragraph has no 久服 clause, and so carries no cultivation status of the substance (*Shennong bencao jing*, Sun Xingyan ed. 1799, lower classic, entry 郁李仁 *yuliren*, `Sources/SBJ_Shennong/text/Shennong_SunXingyan_1799_daizhige.txt` line 3653). The frame is a warning, not a promise. The other layers add none.

**Practitioner's note.** There is no cultivation practice here, and the tradition's own grading argues against long use. The entry is a drug for water swelling and for the root's dental use, with no preparation and no dose.

### Contraindications

The preface warns that lower-class drugs are mostly toxic and not for long use. The Codex read no safety information for this drug in particular, and none for pregnancy, children or other medicines. Rosaceous kernels in general can release cyanide, and no preparation or dose is known here, so no kernel should be eaten on the strength of this entry.
