# Qayqahan — قيقهن (*qayqahan*, as printed)

## *A gum of foul taste brought from the land of the Arabs; some claimed it is sandarac, which the Canon says is not established; no modern identification is offered here (confidence low)* | *(Volume I — Plant-Based)*

**Tradition Tags:** CM

---

### Origin & Tradition

**Single-text entry.** Named only by Avicenna's *Canon* among the texts on disk. Source file: `Sources/CM_Avicenna/text/IbnSina_Qanun_OpenITI_Shamela0010706.txt`.

**Locus (CM).** *Canon* Book II, entry *qayqahan* (قيقهن), heading at Arabic text line 17593; entry to line 17601; the next heading is *quṭn* (line 17602).

**What it is (CM).** 'A gum of repellent taste, brought from the land of the Arabs. Some claimed it is *sandarūs*, and that is not established; one also fumigates with it together with myrrh and *mayʿa*' (lines 17594-17595). **Identity doubt:** the Canon rejects the sandarac equation. This Codex's Sandarac entry (I_1456, from the Canon entry *sandarūs*) is therefore kept separate. [USER: verify]

**What the text says (CM).** Actions: 'a slight agglutinating power' (فيه تغرية يسيرة; line 17595). Cosmetic: it cleans the marks of ulcers quickly, and it has an emaciating power if three-quarters of a dirham (ثلاثة أرباع درهم) is drunk each day with oxymel (*sikanjabīn*) or water (lines 17595-17596). Head: 'nothing equals it in removing toothache and the falling of the gums'. Eye: it polishes the sight. Chest: it helps asthma with honey water, 'wrestlers use it'. Digestive: drunk for three days with oxymel it emaciates the spleen greatly (lines 17596-17601).

### Active Components

The text names no constituent. In general terms gum-resins contain polysaccharides and resin acids; nothing is known here of this material. [USER: verify]

### Effect & Use Case in Kenetica

Historical record only. The entry is a tooth and gum remedy, a spleen-reducing drink and an emaciating drink, with a wrestlers' use. It is not a recommended practice, and the substance is not identified.

### Preparation & Dosing

The Canon gives one dose: three-quarters of a dirham daily with oxymel or water (lines 17595-17596; the *dirham* is not converted), and a three-day drink with oxymel for the spleen. Fumigation with myrrh and *mayʿa* is also named. [USER: verify]

### Traditional Energetic Classification

**Unani / Avicenna (CM).** No degree of heat or dryness is printed. 'A slight agglutinating power' (line 17595) is the only quality.

### Cultivation Register

**Unani / Avicenna (CM).** *(b) Framed.* The entry is a drug-by-drug list of qualities and uses inside the *Canon*'s materia medica. It assigns no cultivation status and says nothing of innate heat, radical moisture or the *ruh*. The athletic use ('wrestlers use it') is a body regimen, not a cultivation status. Nothing is said of the faculties.

**Practitioner's note.** A bitter gum for the teeth and the spleen, with an emaciating dose. The text rejects the sandarac equation and supports no cultivation use.

### Functional–Anatomical Correlate

Qayqahan, a bitter gum the Canon says is brought from the land of the Arabs, is described as having a slight agglutinating power, which is the only quality given, so no degree of heat or dryness is mapped. The Canon places its actions in the mouth, the teeth and gums, the eye and the chest. It is said to be the best thing for toothache and falling of the gums, which maps to the teeth and gingiva. It is said to clean the marks of ulcers and to polish the sight, which maps to the eye. With honey water it is said to help asthma, which maps to the airways and lungs. Drunk daily with oxymel or water it is said to emaciate the body, and drunk for three days with oxymel it is said to greatly reduce the spleen. Wrestlers are said to use it, which the text gives as a body regimen. The text rejects the sandarac identity, and the gum is not identified.

### Contraindications

The text itself calls the drink emaciating and says it wastes the spleen greatly. Do not ingest a gum of unknown species. No contraindication is printed. [USER: verify]
