**Named only by the Sushruta Samhita among the texts on disk, under this transliteration.** This is a single-text entry. The row began on 13 October as an unverified placeholder taken from the group list in chapter 38; this entry replaces it. The names.csv guess was "unresolved (unknown)"; that stands. A search of all the Sushruta text on disk (the chapter files under `Sources/SS_Sushruta/text/` and Bhishagratna's three volumes) for the name found only the group list below.

**Sushruta Samhita (SS).** Chapter 38 of the *Sutrasthana* is the chapter "which deals with the general classification of drugs according to their therapeutical properties (Dravya-Sangrahaniya-madhyaryam)" and says that "these drugs are usually made into thirty-seven different groups (Gana)" (Vol. I lines 15343 to 15350; `text/sutra/SS_Sutra_ch38.txt` lines 3 to 9). The drug is placed there by group, and a group's virtue is stated for the group as a whole.

1. **Group list.** *Sutrasthana* ch.38, the Eladi group (`Sources/SS_Sushruta/text/Bhishagratna_Vol1_djvu.txt` lines 15507 to 15513; `text/sutra/SS_Sutra_ch38.txt` line 166). Bhishagratna prints: "The group of medicinal drugs known as the Eladi-Gana consists of Ela, Tagara, Kushtha, Mansi, Dhyamaka, Tvaka, Patra, Naga-pushpa, Priyangu, Harenuka, Vyaghranakha, Shukti, Chanda, Sthauneyaka, Shriveshtaka, Chocha, Choraka, Valaka, Guggulu, Sarjarasa, Turushka, Kunduruka, Aguru, Sprikka, Ushira, Bhadradaru, Kumkuma, Punnaga and Keshara." (Obvious OCR damage in the e-text, such as "Clianda" for Chanda, is repaired here; the name Dhyamaka is as printed.) It stands fifth, after Mansi.
2. **The group's virtue.** The metrical text gives the virtue of the group as a whole: "subduing the action of Vayu and Kapham and in neutralising the effects of poison. It is a cosmetic and arrests the eruption of pimples and other vegetations on the skin such as rash, urticaria etc. and checks the itching sensation incidental thereto" (lines 15514 to 15521). This is the group's virtue, not a statement about Dhyamaka alone.

The group list is the only place found. No description of the plant, no part used, and no rasa or potency was found, and the glossary of Bhishagratna's Vol. III did not return the name. ### Cultivation Register

**Sushruta Samhita (SS).** *(b) Framed.* The name is attested in a chapter 38 group list, which classifies drugs by the use of the whole group (*Dravya-Sangrahaniya*). Chapter 38 is not a *rasayana* or *vajikarana* chapter, so the text assigns this drug no cultivation status; it carries a therapeutic one only, and only at the level of its group. The concepts of *ojas*, *rasayana* and *vajikarana* are not claimed for it. The Eladi group is described as a cosmetic and anti-itch group; that is a skin virtue, not a *rasayana* one.

**Practitioner's note.** An unidentified name in a group list. Nothing supports cultivation use, and without an identification nothing can be gathered or used. A cultivator reads it as a name in a record.

### Contraindications

- **Identity.** The plant is unidentified. Do not substitute any herb for it.
- **The conditions named.** Skin eruptions, itching, poisoning and disorders of Vayu and Kapha are named for the group; poisoning is an emergency. These are medical conditions; use proper care.
