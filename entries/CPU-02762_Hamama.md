# Hamama — حماما (Avicenna, *Canon* Book II)

## *Ḥamāmā: Avicenna's Arabic form of Dioscorides' *amomon*, a clustered woody shrub with large broad leaves, a gold-coloured small flower and fragrant ruby-coloured wood, best from Armenia. The Canon's description matches *De materia medica* 1-14 (AMOMON) in the English on disk; which plant that is remains unsettled, and the translator offers several (confidence medium for the match, low for the plant)* | *(Volume I — Plant-Based)*

**Avicenna, Canon (CM).** The entry is *ḥamāmā* (*Canon* Book II, Arabic text lines 12919-12945).

*What it is.* الماهية: قال ديسقوريدوس: هي شجرة كأنها عنقود من خشب مشتبك بعضه ببعض وله ورق كبار عراض (12920-12921): "Dioscorides said: a tree as if a cluster of wood interwoven, with large broad leaves"; a small flower "gold in colour" and wood "the colour of ruby (*yāqūt*), sweet of smell" (12922-12923); a damp-place kind that is weaker and greener, with wood in splinters and a smell like rue (12923-12925). Compare Dioscorides in English on disk (line 3672): "a little shrub winding out of the wood within itself", "the best is brought out of Armenia", "resembling origanum in its smell". Avicenna also repeats Dioscorides on adulteration with *armūmīs*, which resembles it but has no smell or fruit and grows in Armenia (12934-12935).

*Choice.* الاختيار (12927-12935): the best is the first, golden, fresh, bitter, sweet of smell; the green-wood kind is poor and weak of smell; Dioscorides: the best is white or reddish, full of seed like clusters, heavy of smell without rankness; test it by crumbling.

*Temperament and actions.* الطبع: حار يابس في الثانية (12936): "hot and dry in the second". It thins and ripens; has astringency; its strength is like that of *wajj* (sweet flag, our gloss) (12936-12937).

*Other uses.* It ripens hot swellings (12937). *Head:* يثقل الرأس ويصدع وينوم (12938): "it burdens the head, gives headache, and induces sleep"; some said that when painted on the forehead it removes headache; "it is among the intoxicants and soporifics" (12938-12939). *Eye:* a decoction as a wash for hot ophthalmia (12939). *Chest:* for cold pleurisy (12940). *Digestive organs:* opens liver obstructions, a decoction drunk for liver complaints, more digesting than *wajj* (12942-12943). *Excretory organs:* it brings the menses, helps womb pain and ulcers, a sitting-bath in its decoction for kidney pain, and swellings of the viscera (12943-12944). *Poisons:* applied with *bādharūj* (basil-type, our gloss) it helps scorpion stings (12945).

### Effect & Use Case in Kenetica

**Historical record only. Not a practice.** The Canon records an aromatic, hot, dry wood or shrub used to ripen swellings, wash the eye, help liver and womb complaints and sting wounds, with sleep-inducing and head-burdening effects. The plant is not firmly identified. The Codex gives no protocol and does not recommend this material.

### Functional–Anatomical Correlate

The Canon gives this aromatic, hot and dry plant in the second degree, with astringency, and says it thins and ripens. Its actions reach the head, eye, chest, liver, womb and kidneys. For the head the passage says it burdens the head, gives headache and induces sleep, and is among the intoxicants and soporifics, while painting it on the forehead is said to remove headache. For the eye, a decoction is a wash for hot ophthalmia. For the chest, it is used for cold pleurisy. For the liver, it opens obstructions when drunk as a decoction, and is more digesting than wajj. For the womb and excretory organs, it is said to bring the menses, help womb pain and ulcers, and act as a sitting-bath for kidney pain. It is also said to ripen hot swellings and, with basil, to help scorpion stings. The plant is not firmly identified, and the Codex does not recommend it.

### Contraindications

- **Sedative / intoxicant.** The source says it induces sleep and burdens the head; avoid.
- **Pregnancy.** The source says it moves the menses; avoid. [USER: verify]
- **Identity.** Not firmly identified; do not substitute any cardamom or *Amomum*. [USER: verify]
