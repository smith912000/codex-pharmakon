Dioscorides describes it as "a tender shrubby herb, bringing out smooth stalks, very jagged leaves all around, and yellowish flowers strongly shining with an eye", which "grows in towns" and has stalks that "are eaten as vegetables". Its flowers, "pounded into small pieces with wax ointment", are "said to dissolve steatomata", and "it gives the jaundiced a good colour". A parenthetical sentence adds that the flowers "are hung around the neck, being good for averting women witches and all enchantments". None of this concerns a *Fritillaria*.
