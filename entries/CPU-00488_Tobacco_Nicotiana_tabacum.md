# Tobacco — *picietl* (Nahuatl), Sacred Tobacco, *mapacho* (Amazonian), Common Tobacco

## *Nicotiana tabacum* L. | Family: Solanaceae

**Tradition Tags:** TCM (Chinese medicine), BM (Badianus), SA (S.America)
**Energetic Classification:** Hot and dry in the third to fourth degree; acrid, narcotic-stimulant, and poisonous in excess (Galenic/post-Columbian humoral reading — an intensely heating, purging, stupefying herb)
**Training Phase:** Integration (advanced, hazard-gated) — never Foundation

---

> ⚠️ **TOXIC · ADDICTIVE · CARCINOGENIC WHEN COMBUSTED.** Nicotine is a potent alkaloid, lethal in overdose, powerfully addictive, and a developmental neurotoxin. This entry documents traditional and ceremonial context; it is **not** an endorsement of use. See Contraindications, which govern this entry.

---

### Origin & Tradition

**Badianus (BM):** The *Libellus de Medicinalibus Indorum Herbis* (1552, the Badianus Codex) — the Aztec herbal compiled by Martín de la Cruz and translated by Juan Badiano — records *picietl* (tobacco) among the Nahua materia medica, used in poultices, fumigations, and compound remedies for pain, fatigue, and swelling. Tobacco held ritual as well as medical standing in Mesoamerica, offered to deities and used by healers.

**South American (Indigenous, attested):** Across Amazonia tobacco (*mapacho*, strong *N. tabacum* and *N. rustica*) is a central shamanic plant — taken as smoke, snuff, licked paste (*ambil*), or liquid, used by *tabaqueros* for purification, protection, divinatory work, and as a companion plant in ceremonial contexts. Its status as an entheogen and "master plant" in these traditions is genuine and well documented ethnographically. This use is embedded in trained ritual specialist practice and carries real danger outside it.

**Shennong tradition (by later diffusion):** Tobacco reached China only after the Columbian exchange; it was subsequently absorbed into later Chinese herbal practice as a warming, acrid, insecticidal and phlegm-moving agent. It is **not** part of the original Han *Shennong Bencao Jing* and is flagged here as a post-contact addition, not an ancient Chinese simple.

**Doctrinal & Symbolic Layer:** Tobacco's doctrine is the doctrine of the guarded threshold — a plant of protection, purge, and communion whose power is inseparable from its poison. In Amazonian cosmology it is a disciplining, boundary-setting ally; pharmacologically, nicotine is an agonist at nicotinic acetylcholine receptors, producing the arousal, focus, and nausea/purging that ceremonial use both seeks and controls. The symbolism of a plant that must be handled by the trained and can kill the unready is, unusually, literally accurate: the same alkaloid that concentrates attention is a fast, potentially fatal neurotoxin. It is placed in **Integration** only as a studied, hazard-gated subject — its more honest place in any responsible practice is as a plant to understand, not to consume.

### Active Components

- **Nicotine** — the principal alkaloid; nicotinic acetylcholine receptor agonist; stimulant, addictive, and acutely toxic; also a natural insecticide.
- **Nornicotine, anabasine, anatabine** — minor pyridine alkaloids.
- **Harman and norharman (β-carbolines)** — MAO-inhibitory constituents relevant to the psychoactive/addictive profile of smoke.
- **Combustion products** — tar, polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons, nitrosamines, carbon monoxide: the carcinogenic and cardiovascular hazard of smoked tobacco, distinct from the alkaloid itself.

### Effect & Use Case in Kenetica

In Kenetica, tobacco is treated as an **object of study and extreme caution, not a training agent.** Its ceremonial South American context is acknowledged as real and as belonging strictly to trained ritual lineage practice. There is **no recommended self-administration protocol** in this system. Where it appears at the Integration phase, it is as a subject for understanding the boundary between medicine and poison, agonist and toxin — the archetype of a plant whose dose *is* its danger. Any legitimate ceremonial engagement belongs to established traditional-specialist settings, not to individual experimentation.

### Scientific Correlation

According to PubMed:

- Archie, Sharma, Burks & Abbruscato (2022), *NeuroToxicology* — reviews the neurovascular toxicity of nicotine and tobacco smoke, detailing nicotine's disruption of blood–brain-barrier permeability, nicotinic-receptor and dopaminergic effects, and its role in addiction, with sex- and age-dependent pharmacokinetics. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuro.2022.02.002)
- England, Aagaard, Bloch et al. (2017), *Neuroscience & Biobehavioral Reviews* — a transdisciplinary synthesis concluding that nicotine exposure in pregnancy and adolescence causes lasting harm (impaired pulmonary and cardiorespiratory function, auditory-processing and cognitive/behavioural deficits) and has an addiction-priming effect, warranting protective public-health measures. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neubiorev.2016.11.013)

**Evidence grade:** The harms (addiction, neurotoxicity, developmental toxicity, and — for combusted tobacco — carcinogenicity and cardiovascular disease) are among the best-established findings in all of medicine. The ceremonial/ethnobotanical context is well documented anthropologically. No credible evidence supports recreational or self-directed medicinal use as safe.

### Traditional Energetic Classification

- **TCM (post-contact reading):** warm/hot, acrid, slightly toxic; moves qi, transforms phlegm, kills parasites — used cautiously and topically in later practice.
- **Ayurveda (by analogy):** *rasa* katu-tikta (pungent-bitter); *guna* sharp, hot, penetrating; *virya* ushna (heating); *vipaka* pungent. Strongly *kapha*- and *vata*-disturbing; classed with intoxicant-poisons requiring specialist handling.
- **Galenic/Unani (post-Columbian):** hot and dry, high degree; narcotic-acrid, purging, stupefying — grouped with the strong, dangerous simples.

### Contraindications

**This plant is toxic and addictive. Blanket cautions apply.**

- **Nicotine is lethal in overdose** (oral, and via skin absorption). Keep all tobacco and nicotine products away from children and animals; ingestion is a poisoning emergency (contact poison control).
- **Highly addictive** — nicotine dependence is rapid and durable; any repeated use risks addiction.
- **Combusted tobacco is carcinogenic** and causes cardiovascular and pulmonary disease; there is no safe level of smoking.
- **Absolutely contraindicated in pregnancy, lactation, childhood, and adolescence** — documented developmental neurotoxicity and lasting harm.
- Contraindicated with **cardiovascular disease, arrhythmia, uncontrolled hypertension**, and alongside stimulants or MAO-active agents.
- **Green tobacco sickness**: nicotine poisoning from skin contact with plant material — a hazard for topical and agricultural exposure.
- Kenetica supplies **no self-administration dose**. Ceremonial use, where culturally legitimate, belongs solely to trained traditional specialists.
