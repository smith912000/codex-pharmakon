# Mimosa rubicanlis — an unread Sanskrit name beginning "Ra-" (Sushruta Samhita, Bhishagratna's Appendix No. 3); "rubicanlis" is an OCR misprint

## *Mimosa rubicaulis Lam., a thorny climbing or scrambling shrub of the legume family, the Latin that Bhishagratna's Appendix No. 3 ("Names of Drugs, with Latin synonyms", Vol. III) prints as OCR "Mimosa Rubicanlis." (line 32704), the "n" being a misreading of "u" ("rubicaulis"). The Sanskrit name it stands against is, by counting down the OCR columns, a name the OCR prints as "RsUa" (line 32657) and that cannot be read from the file; confidence low* | *(Volume I — Plant-Based)*

### Origin & Tradition

**Named only by the Sushruta text (SS); this entry rests on one line of one text, Bhishagratna's English translation of the Sushruta Samhita (Vol. III, Appendix No. 3), and on a Latin name that comes from the translator's appendix and not from the chapters.** The chapters are in `Sources/SS_Sushruta/text/` and the appendix is in `Bhishagratna_Vol3_djvu.txt`. The row was opened on 13 October from the extraction (`Sources/SS_Sushruta/extract/names.csv`), where the Latin line is kept as "not paired to a Sanskrit name (OCR column split)". The row name "Mimosa rubicanlis" repeats the OCR error; the Codex corrects the spelling in the identification line only. The species has not been checked against a current plant list. [USER: verify]

**The pairing, by counting.** The page that carries this Latin line has a run of Sanskrit names from "Rajadana" to "Rodhra" (lines 32628–32676) and, after them, the Latin run from "Mimusops Indica." (line 32680). Near the end of the run the Sanskrit names are "Rakta-Chandana", "Rakta-Chitraka", "Rakta-Eranda", "Rakta-Kanchana", "Rakta-kuruntaka", "Rakta-Sali", "Raktotpala", "**RsUa**" (line 32657), "Ra'snst" (OCR for Rasna), "Rasona", "Rasala" and "Rasanjana". The Latin entries in step with them are "Pterocarpus Santalinus", "Plumbago Rosea", "Ricinus Communis", "Bauhimia Variegata", "Barlaria Cristola", "Oriza Sativa", "Nymphoea Rubra", "**Mimosa Rubicanlis.**" (line 32704), "Vanda Roxburghii", "Allium Sativum. Garlic", "Mangifera Indica. Mango" and "Extract of Berberis Asiatica". These agree name by name where the Sanskrit is clear: Rakta-chandana with red sandalwood, Rakta-chitraka with red leadwort, Rakta-eranda with castor, Rakta-kanchana with *Bauhinia*, Rakta-sali with rice, Raktotpala with the red water-lily, Rasna with *Vanda*, Rasona with garlic, Rasala with mango, Rasanjana with the extract of barberry. The one name in the run that the OCR does not give in a readable form, "RsUa", therefore stands against *Mimosa rubicaulis*. The Codex does not try to restore the word. A name beginning "Ra" and ending in "a", between Raktotpala and Rasna in an alphabetical list, is all that can be said. [USER: verify against the page image]
