# Blyxa octandra — Saivala (Sushruta Samhita, glossary identification "Blyxa Octandra")
## *Blyxa octandra (Roxb.) Planch. ex Thwaites, an aquatic plant; the identification is Bhishagratna's glossary entry read at the id line, with the pairing to the name Saivala inferred from column order, and is therefore of medium confidence* | *(Volume I — Plant-Based)*

**Tradition Tags:** SS

---

### Origin & Tradition

**Single-text entry.** Only the *Sushruta Samhita* (Bhishagratna) names it among the texts on disk. The row began on 8 November 2026 as an unverified placeholder from the Appendix 3 glossary key "Blyxa Octandra"; this entry replaces it.

**Sushruta (SS), glossary.** In `Sources/SS_Sushruta/text/Bhishagratna_Vol3_djvu.txt` the identification column prints ", Blyxa Octandra." at line 32812, followed by "Sectona grandis." (32814), "See Kutaja." (32816), "Streblus asper." (32818), "Shorea robusta." (32820) and "Bomba[x] malabaricum." (32822). The name column begins its S' names with "S'aivala" (line 32735, OCR "S'aivs^la"), then continues through the S' names to "Sephalika" (line 32806). Blyxa octandra is the first identification of the block and Saivala the first name of the block, so the Codex reads the pair as Saivala = Blyxa octandra. This is an inference from order, not a printed line; the OCR is damaged. [USER: verify against the printed page.] The line immediately before the S' names (32726) reads "A species of Lichen", which belongs to the preceding entry and is not used here.

**Sushruta (SS), uses read.** Shaivala is named in two places:
- **Sutra ch.39** (`Sources/SS_Sushruta/text/sutra/SS_Sutra_ch39.txt` line 106): in a list ("Manjishtha, Payasya, Vidari, Shatavari, Gundra, Shaivala, Kahlara, Kumuda, Utpala, Kadali, Kandali, Durva, Murva, etc."), followed by the groups Kakolyadi, Sarivadi, Anjanadi, Utpaladi, Nyagrodhadi and Trina-Panchamula "generally", in a passage on drugs (lines 105-109).
- **Chikitsa ch.05** (`Sources/SS_Sushruta/text/chikitsa/SS_Chikitsa_ch05.txt` line 206): S'aivala is one of the ingredients of a plaster (pradeha), "composed of S'ali, Shashtika, Nala, Vanjula, Talisha, Shrigataka, Galodya, Haridra, Gairika, S'aivala, Padma-kashtha, leaves of padma (lotus), pasted with Dhanyamla and mixed with clarified butter", applied lukewarm to the affected part, including in Vata-rakta (lines 205-208).

Whether the plant in these passages is Blyxa, or the pond-weed or algae that other works call shaivala, is not settled by the passages. [USER: verify]

### Effect & Use Case in Kenetica

**Historical record only. Not a practice.** The plant appears as an ingredient of a cooling-type plaster list in Chikitsa ch.05 and in a drug list in Sutra ch.39; neither passage assigns a separate action to it. The Codex does not recommend any use.

### Preparation & Dosing

The only preparation read is the plaster in Chikitsa ch.05: the ingredients pasted with Dhanyamla (fermented gruel), mixed with clarified butter and applied lukewarm. No quantity is given. No dose is given in the sources retrieved. [USER: verify]

### Scientific Correlation

General, well-established facts only. Blyxa species are small submerged or emergent freshwater plants of tropical Asia. No clinical studies were retrieved for this entry. [USER: verify]
