### Active Components

Amber is fossilised tree resin, a hard, translucent, yellow to brown organic solid that is not a mineral in the strict sense. Baltic amber (succinite) is known to contain succinic acid and resin-derived terpenoid polymers; it burns with a resinous smell and becomes electrically charged when rubbed. The sources read give no composition.

### Scientific Correlation

Amber is valued as a gem and for jewellery and is not a recognised medicine in current practice. Beads and necklaces worn by small children are a recognised strangulation and choking hazard. No clinical studies were retrieved for this entry. [USER: verify]
