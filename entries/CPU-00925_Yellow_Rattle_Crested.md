**Greek — Dioscorides (DMM).** The text is `Sources/DMM_DioscoRides/text/Dioscorides_De_Materia_Medica_2000_English.txt` (Osbaldeston and Wood 2000), and the name index is `Sources/DMM_DioscoRides/extract/names.csv`. A search for *Rhinanthus*, "rattle", "cockscomb", "crest" and *crista* found no chapter identified as yellow rattle. Four chapters were attached to this row by the genus-level extraction (`EXTRACTION_GAPS.md`, line 285), and none is about *Rhinanthus*:

- **1-2 AKORON** (line 3547): the translator's identifications are sweet flag and yellow flag iris (*Iris pseudacorus*, "Yellow Flag").
- **1-4 KUPEIROS** (line 3569): cyperus, yellow nutsedge.
- **2-135 GONGOLE AGRIOE** (line 5666): wild turnip, winter cress ("Yellow Rocket") and rampion.
- **2-151 ANDRACHNE AGRIA** (line 5813): "Yellow Portulaca".

They share the word "yellow" with the common name and nothing else.

One passage does print the Latin name element *crista*. Under **4-60 PERISTEREON ORTHOS** (heading at line 8522; the translator's identification is vervain, hedge mustard, "Verbena recta" and lemon verbena) the list of names closes: "the Romans, crista gallinacea, and some, ferrea, trixalis, exupera, or herba sanguinalis" (line 8526). The synonym index lists "crista gallinacea 600" (line 11028). This is a Roman synonym ("hen's crest") for the plant of that chapter, so it attests a name resemblance only. It does not attest *Rhinanthus*. The chapter describes a herb of watery places with whitish cut leaves, and it is not a hemiparasite of meadows.

No Dioscorides chapter for this plant was found.
