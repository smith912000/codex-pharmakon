# Cup fungus — ghūshna (غوشنة)

## *A truffle-like fungus the Canon says dries to a cartilage-like body and has the shape of a cup on a small wrinkled paunch; it is used to wash cloth and eaten in sour dishes; the species is not identified and the first-pass note of a cup fungus is a descriptive reading only; confidence low; the spine has Truffle (CPU-01280, Avicenna and Dioscorides) and this is not merged with it* | *(Volume IV — Fungal/Algal, shelved in Volume I)*
**Tradition Tags:** CM

---

### Origin & Tradition

**Named only by Avicenna's *Canon* among the texts on disk.** This is a single-text entry. Book II, entry *ghūshna*, Arabic text from line 19584 of `Sources/CM_Avicenna/text/IbnSina_Qanun_OpenITI_Shamela0010706.txt`. A search of the other texts on disk was not run beyond the spine check. 

**Unani / Avicenna (CM).** What it is: "a genus of the truffles (*kamʾa*) and fungi (*faṭar*), dried and it contracts like cartilage (*ghuḍrūf*); its shape is the shape of a cup on a small wrinkled paunch (*karsh*); clothes are washed with it, and it is eaten in sour dishes (*ḥumūḍāt*), and it has a pleasure like the pleasure of cartilages and more." Temperament: "it is not in the coldness of the other truffles." Properties: "it is not bad in humour like the truffles, but in its nature is a leavening (*takhmīr*) or an alkalinity (*qalawiyya*)."

**Overlap.** The spine's *Truffle* (I_871, CPU-01280) is a general truffle entry that includes Avicenna; this one is the Canon's own heading and is kept apart. [USER: verify]

**Identity doubt.** The cup shape on a wrinkled stalk could be a cup fungus or other; the text does not choose. [USER: verify]

### Active Components

None is given in the text. Fungi in general are rich in water and chitin; this is general, not from the text.

### Effect & Use Case in Kenetica

**Historical record only. Not a practice.** The text records a fungus used as a washing agent for cloth and eaten in sour dishes. Wild fungi are not safe to eat on the strength of this entry.

### Preparation & Dosing

Used dried to wash clothes; eaten in sour dishes. No dose is given in the sources retrieved. [USER: verify]

General fact only. No clinical studies were retrieved for this entry. [USER: verify]

### Traditional Energetic Classification

"Not in the coldness of the other truffles"; not bad in humour; a leavening or alkaline nature.

### Cultivation Register

**Unani / Avicenna (CM).** No cultivation framing is attested. **(c) Absent.** The *Canon* entry is organised by organ and complaint and says nothing here about innate heat, radical moisture or the *ruh*. 

**Practitioner's note.** Nothing to practise. It is a minor fungus used for washing and as a food.

### Contraindications

- Wild fungi can be deadly; the species here is not identified.
- Do not eat any wild fungus on the strength of this entry.
