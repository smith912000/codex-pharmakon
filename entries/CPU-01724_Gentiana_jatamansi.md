### Origin & Tradition

**Named only by the Tibetan texts (FMT), and only by one line of one text, which on a close reading is not a binomial.** No other text read for this entry names the plant. The Tibetan texts are in `Sources/FMT_Tibetan/text/`. A search of the Dioscorides, Shennong (Yang 1998), Ebers, Assyrian and Sushruta texts on disk (including the three Bhishagratna volumes) for the genus name *Gentiana* found Dioscorides 3-3 GENTIANE (line 6541, the translator's glosses *Gentiana lutea* and Yellow Gentian) and Yang 1998's Long Dan (line 1936) and Qin Jiao (line 2494); none is *Gentiana jatamansi*. Clark 1995 does not print "jatamansi" under *Gentiana* (the Latin-name index of Clark was searched). **Tibetan — rGyud-bzhi (FMT).** *Men-Tsee-Khang 2011, glossary, line 5657.* `MenTseeKhang_2011_Root_and_Explanatory_Tantra.txt`. The line reads "Gentiana Jatamansi Meadow grass". It stands in the run that follows the "P" block's scientific names and precedes the "R" block (the letter "R" is at line 5654). The lines around it are English names: "Zeodany" (line 5656), "Pokeberry" (5659), "Tibetan lancea Silk-cotton tree Motherwort Black pepper Wormwood ashes" (5661), "Long pepper" (5662), "Sal tree resin" (5663), "Orchid" (5665), and dashes where an entry has no English name. That is the English column of the "P" block, not a scientific-name column.

The "P" block (from line 5578) lists sixteen phonetic names, "Pagoe", "Pang gyen karpo", "Pangpoe", "Pang tsi dowo", "Parpata", "Pawo karpo", "Pawo serpo", "Payak", "Pema gesar", "Phangma", "Phowari", "Phur thal", "Pipiling", "Poekar", "Pukhar mula" and "Pushel", and sixteen scientific names in the same order, including *Curcuma zedoaria* for "Pagoe" (line 5582), *Gentiana algida* for "Pang gyen karpo" (line 5584) and *Nardostachys jatamansi* for "Pangpoe" (line 5590). Counting the English cells of the run, "Zeodany" is the first (it fits *Curcuma zedoaria*), "Gentiana" the second (it fits "Pang gyen karpo"), "Jatamansi" the third (it fits "Pangpoe", *Nardostachys jatamansi*), "Meadow grass" the fourth, a dash the fifth and "Pokeberry" the sixth (it fits "Pawo karpo", *Phytolacca acinosa*, line 5600), and the sixteenth is "Orchid" (it fits "Pushel", *Dendrobium*, line 5652). The count of sixteen English cells matches the count of sixteen names. On that count, the words "Gentiana" and "Jatamansi" in line 5657 are two separate English cells, for two separate drugs, and the extraction joined them into one binomial. This reading is by position and was not printed in one row. [USER: verify]

On this reading there is no Tibetan drug named *Gentiana jatamansi* in the sources read, and no name, action, taste or preparation attaches to it. The Tibetan drug *spang spos* (*Nardostachys jatamansi*, line 5588 and 5590) already has the spine row CPU-00001 (I_137, Jatamansi), and *Gentiana algida* is covered in I_1286.

**Overlap.** I_137 (Jatamansi, *Nardostachys*) and the entry on *Gentiana algida* in this batch (I_1286) are the entries that these two cells belong to on the count above. Nothing is merged.
