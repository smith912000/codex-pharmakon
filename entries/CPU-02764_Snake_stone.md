### Effect & Use Case in Kenetica

**Historical record only. Not a practice.** The Canon records an amulet stone reported against snakebite on a Galenic hearsay. It is not a practice, and a hung stone gives no protection against venom. The Codex gives no protocol and does not recommend this material.

### Preparation & Dosing

Wearing it hung on the body (*taʿlīqan*, line 13446). No dose is given in the sources retrieved. [USER: verify]

### Scientific Correlation

General fact only: a stone worn on the body does not neutralise venom. Snakebite needs urgent medical care. No clinical studies were retrieved for this entry. [USER: verify]
