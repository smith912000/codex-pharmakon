### Origin & Tradition

**Shennong lineage (SBJ) — the six Zhī:** The *Shén Nóng Běncǎo Jīng*, the foundational Chinese materia medica, lists a set of six coloured "Zhī" (芝) — the auspicious, longevity-associated bracket fungi — organised by colour and by the Five-Phase/organ system:
- **Qīng Zhī (青芝)** — green/azure, associated with the Liver;
- **Chì Zhī (赤芝)** — red, Heart (this is the one most confidently identified with modern *Ganoderma lucidum*);
- **Huáng Zhī (黄芝)** — yellow, Spleen;
- **Bái Zhī (白芝)** — white, Lung;
- **Hēi Zhī (黑芝)** — black, Kidney;
- **Zǐ Zhī (紫芝)** — purple, often identified with *Ganoderma sinense*.

*Qīng Zhī*, the green Zhī, was placed in the "superior" (non-toxic, longevity-promoting, spirit-benefiting) class and was said to brighten the eyes, calm the *hún* (ethereal soul) and the spirit, and — in the aspirational language of the text — to lighten the body and extend life with long use.

**Honest identity caveat — this entry's central uncertainty:** the "six Zhī" are a *cosmological colour scheme* mapped onto real fungi, and the botanical identity of several of them is genuinely unresolved. Only the red Zhī (*Ganoderma lucidum*) and the purple Zhī (*Ganoderma sinense*) are reasonably well identified with named modern species. **The species behind *Qīng Zhī* (green Zhī) is historically uncertain** — it may denote a distinct *Ganoderma* taxon, a colour-graded specimen, or a partly symbolic category. This entry therefore treats the modern pharmacology as *genus-level Ganoderma* evidence, not as verified data on a specific "green" species. Species: `[USER: verify]`.

**Doctrinal & Symbolic Layer — The Colour That Names the Organ:** The six Zhī are the clearest surviving case of a materia medica organised by *correspondence rather than by specimen*: green→Liver, red→Heart, and so on around the Five-Phase wheel. *Qīng Zhī* is the Liver-Wood fungus — its green is the colour of spring, of the *hún*, of the eyes the Liver is said to open into. In Kenetica this is read frankly as a **doctrinal placeholder**: a slot in a system that later fungal pharmacology only partly fills. We do not pretend the green Zhī has a confirmed unique chemistry; we honour the slot and attach the honest, genus-level science.

*(Genus-level Ganoderma constituents — not specific to a verified "green" species.)*

- **Triterpenoids (ganoderic acids and relatives)** — bitter tetracyclic/pentacyclic triterpenes; associated with hepatoprotective, anti-inflammatory, and antioxidant actions.
- **Polysaccharides (β-glucans)** — high-molecular-weight immunomodulatory carbohydrates.
- **Peptidoglycans / polysaccharide-peptides** — additional immunomodulatory fractions.
- **Phenols** — antioxidant constituents.
- Documented genus-level actions: immunomodulation, antioxidant, antimicrobial, anti-aging/lifespan-related, and anticancer activity in preclinical models.

### Scientific Correlation

Evidence grade: **preclinical, and genus-level only** (no verified study on an authenticated "green Zhī"). According to PubMed:

- **Phytochemistry & therapeutic potential (review).** *Ganoderma* species produce polysaccharides, phenols, and triterpenes largely responsible for their therapeutic properties, with demonstrated immunomodulatory, antiaging, antimicrobial, and anticancer activities across multiple species — though the authors note the challenge of translating fungal-metabolite effects to human health benefit (Blundell et al., 2023, *Am J Chin Med*). [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1142/S0192415X23500404)
- **Anti-aging roles (review).** Multiple *Ganoderma* extracts and constituents (e.g., polysaccharide fractions, triterpenes, ganoderic acid C1) have shown lifespan-elongation or related activities in model systems, though the authors stress that confirmed anti-aging components remain a small fraction of those isolated (Wang et al., 2017, *Aging Dis*). [DOI](https://doi.org/10.14336/AD.2017.0410)

These are genus-level, largely preclinical findings. None authenticate or specifically test *Qīng Zhī* as a distinct species. No human clinical claim is made here.

### Contraindications

- **Identity risk:** because the species is unverified, sourcing "green Ganoderma" reliably is not currently possible; any product so labelled should be treated with scrutiny. `[USER: verify]` the actual species supplied.
- **Bleeding / anticoagulation:** Ganoderma triterpenes/polysaccharides may have antiplatelet effects; caution with anticoagulants and before surgery (based on *G. lucidum* data).
- **Immunosuppressant therapy:** immunomodulatory fungi may theoretically interfere; caution in transplant/autoimmune contexts.
- **Pregnancy/lactation:** insufficient data; avoid without supervision.
