**Avicenna, *Canon* of Medicine (CM).** (Canon Book II, entry *Hurṭumān*, Arabic text line 12306–12309). *What it is:* "its grain's strength is the strength of barley, rather it is like the mean between wheat and barley" (حبه قوته قوة الشعير بل هو كالمتوسط بين الحنطة والشعير), and its *sawīq* (parched meal) and *dashīsh* (coarse gruel) are more astringent than the *sawīq* and *dashīsh* of barley. *Actions:* it dries without stinging, and in it is dissolving and astringency together (يجفف بلا لذع وفيه تحليل وقبض معا). That is the whole entry. The vowels of the name are our transliteration. No authority is named.
