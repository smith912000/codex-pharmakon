### Origin & Tradition

**Row key.** `Sources/SS_Sushruta/extract/names.csv` line 553 reads "Mtrisba", appendix line 32235, Latin line 32306, "Araaranthus obraceus.". The Latin is printed once in the appendix; the OCR spelling is garbled ("Araaranthus obraceus"), and the Codex reads it as *Amaranthus oleraceus*, as the spine does. Latin lines are quoted below with obvious OCR slips corrected.

The neighbours fit: Marich with black pepper before, and Markati with "See Suka-simbi" and Markava with *Wedelia calendulacea* after; Manjishtha with *Rubia cordifolia* and Mamsi with *Nardostachys jatamansi* a little earlier. The OCR "Mtrisba" sits between Marich and Markati in alphabetical order, where a name Marisha would stand, but the printed letters do not confirm that reading; the pairing therefore rests on the count and on the position, and the Sanskrit name is not established.
