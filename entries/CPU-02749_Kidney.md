# Kidney — كلية (Avicenna, *Canon* Book II)

## *The kidney of an animal eaten as food (kulya); the Canon names the kid (jady, a young goat) as the best. The identification to kidney meat is the plain reading of the Arabic and is of high confidence* | *(Volume III — Animal)*

**Tradition Tags:** CM

---

### Origin & Tradition

**Named only by Avicenna's *Canon* among the texts on disk, so far as searched.** This is a single-text entry. The spine row attests Avicenna alone (Canon Book II, heading *kulya*, Arabic text line 14307 in `Sources/CM_Avicenna/text/IbnSina_Qanun_OpenITI_Shamela0010706.txt`). The Assyrian entry on animal fat (III_319, CPU-00536) mentions kidney suet; that is a different material and is not merged here.

**Avicenna, Canon (CM).** The entry is *kulya* (*Canon* Book II, Arabic text lines 14307-14310), a food entry, short. "What it is" (الماهية): معروف, "well known" (14308).

*Choice.* The e-text reads الاختيار: أحممصا غذاء كلية الجدي (14308-14309); the word أحممصا is corrupt and not translated here. [USER: verify against another witness.] The clear part is that the kid's kidney is the one singled out; the entry ends its next sentence with وأحمده كلية الجدي (14309): "and the most laudable of them is the kid's kidney".

*Temperament.* الطبع: معتدل إلى اليبس (14309): "moderate, inclining to dryness".

*Qualities (الخواص).* خلطها رديء (14309): "its humour is bad".

*Digestion.* عسر الانهضام زهم بطيء الإنحدار (14310): "hard to digest, greasy (*zahim*), slow to descend". No source author, no preparation and no dose are given.

### Active Components

The passage names no constituent. In general terms kidney is an organ meat rich in protein, iron, B vitamins and purines, and it concentrates some environmental contaminants such as heavy metals. No analytical source was retrieved. [USER: verify]

### Effect & Use Case in Kenetica

**Historical record only. Not a practice.** The Canon treats kidney as a food of poor humour and heavy digestion, with the kid's kidney as the best of a poor lot. It gives no remedy use in the lines read. The Codex gives no protocol.

### Preparation & Dosing

None given. No dose is given in the sources retrieved. [USER: verify]

### Scientific Correlation

General, well-established facts only. Organ meats are nutrient-dense; kidney is high in purines, which matters for gout, and concentrates cadmium and other metals in older animals. No clinical studies were retrieved for this entry. [USER: verify]

### Traditional Energetic Classification

- **CM.** Temperament: moderate (*muʿtadil*), inclining to dryness (14309). Humour: "bad" (14309). Slow, difficult digestion (14310). No degree is printed.

**Practitioner's note.** Nothing to do. By the Canon's own judgement, a food to be eaten sparingly, if at all.

### Contraindications

- **Gout and high uric acid.** Organ meat is purine-rich. [USER: verify]
- **Hygiene.** Cook through; organ meats are a food-poisoning risk if undercooked.
