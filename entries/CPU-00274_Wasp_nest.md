### Active Components

A wasp nest of the paper-wasp kind is a comb of paper-like material, made by the insects from chewed plant fibre mixed with their saliva, which is mainly cellulose and lignin with salivary protein. Old nests may hold the remains of larvae and their silk. The composition of the drug meant by the Classic is unknown. The sources read give no composition.

A wasp nest is an inert paper-like material with no established medical use. Venom-sting allergy can be severe, and old nests may be contaminated by mould, droppings and insect remains. No clinical studies were retrieved for this entry. [USER: verify]

### Contraindications

Wasps sting, and venom allergy can be life-threatening; nests should not be handled by the untrained, and old nests may carry mould and microbes. Wasp-nest products sold as medicines are unstandardised and may be contaminated. The species is not known from the sources read, and the legal status of collecting the nest has not been checked here.
