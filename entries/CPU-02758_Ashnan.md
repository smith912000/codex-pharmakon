# Ashnan — أشنان (Avicenna, *Canon* Book II)

## *Ushnān (*ashnān*): an alkali-bearing plant ash or herb sold in several kinds, the finest white and called "sparrow droppings", one green. **Avicenna, Canon (CM).** The entry is *ushnān* (*Canon* Book II, Arabic text lines 10362-10368).

*What it is.* الماهية: هي أنواع ألطفها الأبيض ويسمى خرء العصافير وأحدها الأخضر (10363-10364): "it has kinds, the finest the white, called *khurʾ al-ʿaṣāfīr* (sparrow droppings), and one of them the green". No plant, habitat or part is described.

*Actions.* الأفعال والخواص: جلاء منق مفتح (10364-10365): "a cleanser (*jallāʾ*), a purifier, an opener".

*Excretory organs.* A half dirham "dissolves difficulty of urine"; five dirhams "drop the child, living or dead"; half a dirham of the Persian to one dirham "brings on the menses"; three dirhams draw off the watery matter of dropsy (the Arabic reads *yasqul* for what is probably *yushil*, a scribal slip in the e-text, our reading) (10365-10367).

*Poisons.* السموم: وزن عشرة دراهم سم قتال ودخان الأخضر منه تنفر عنه الهوام (10367-10368): "ten dirhams is a deadly poison, and the smoke of the green kind makes vermin flee from it".

### Effect & Use Case in Kenetica

**Historical record only. Not a practice.** The Canon records a cleansing alkali material used to pass urine, bring the menses, expel a child and drain dropsy, and warns that ten dirhams kill. It is not a practice and is dangerous. The Codex gives no protocol and does not recommend this material.

### Preparation & Dosing

Only the dose-by-weight statements printed in the passage, all in dirhams, as quoted above (lines 10365-10368). These are the historical record and are not recommendations. **Do not use any dose from this entry.**

### Scientific Correlation

General fact only: plant ashes rich in alkali carbonates are caustic and irritant to the gut and skin, and large amounts are toxic. The species is not identified here. No clinical studies were retrieved for this entry. [USER: verify]

### Functional–Anatomical Correlate

The Canon's entry gives this alkali material actions of cleansing, purifying and opening (jalla, munaqqi, muftahh), and maps it to the urinary organs, the womb and dropsy, with its green kind's smoke said to make vermin flee. For the urinary organs, half a dirham is said to dissolve difficulty of urine. For the womb it is said to bring on the menses at half a dirham of the Persian kind to one dirham. For dropsy, three dirhams are said to draw off the watery matter. At five dirhams the passage says it expels a child, living or dead, which is the abortifacient claim. Ten dirhams is named a deadly poison. No temperament is printed, and the species is not stated. These doses are the historical record only. The Codex does not recommend the material, and no dose from this entry should be used.

### Contraindications

- **TOXIC.** The source gives a lethal weight. Do not ingest.
- **Pregnancy.** The source says it expels the child. Do not use.
- **Identity.** The species is not stated; do not infer a plant. [USER: verify]
