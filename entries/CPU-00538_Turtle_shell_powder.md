### Active Components

None can be stated for the material as the list names it. In general terms, turtle and tortoise shell is bone covered with keratin plates; the bone is mostly calcium phosphate, and calcining (strong heating) converts organic matter to ash and leaves a mineral residue. This is a general statement, not a reading of any tablet.
