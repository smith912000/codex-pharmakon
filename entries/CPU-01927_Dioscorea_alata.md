# Dioscorea alata — Dandalu (OCR "DandsLlu"), Sushruta Samhita Appendix 3

## *Dioscorea alata L. (greater yam, water yam), a twining yam with an edible tuber, as the translator's Latin gloss to the Sanskrit name read in the OCR as "DandsLlu" (Dandalu); the Latin is Bhishagratna's, the pairing with the Sanskrit name is ours from the two OCR columns, and the identification is of medium confidence because the Sanskrit name is garbled and is not found in the main text* | *(Volume I — Plant-Based)*

### Origin & Tradition

**Named in the *Sushruta Samhita* (SS) only through the translator's Appendix No. 3, "Names of drugs with Latin synonyms".** This is a single-text entry. The appendix is printed in two columns, Sanskrit names on the left and Bhishagratna's Latin or English glosses on the right, and the e-text `Sources/SS_Sushruta/text/Bhishagratna_Vol3_djvu.txt` splits the two columns into separate runs of lines. In that file the Sanskrit name stands at line 31565 as "DandsLlu" (the OCR garbles diacritics) and the Latin gloss "Dioscorea  Alata." at line 31595. The extraction file `Sources/SS_Sushruta/extract/names.csv` lists the Latin line as unpaired ("OCR column split"). The pairing here is ours, made by counting entries in the two columns from points where the pairs are unambiguous: the first Latin line of the page block, "Punica Granatuni. Pomegranate.", falls on the grouped names Dadima and Dadimba, and then Dandotpala to Canscora decussata, Danti to Baliospermum Montanum, Darbha to Poa cynosuroides and Dadru-marda to Cassia alata, so that the name before Dandotpala, "DandsLlu", falls on "Dioscorea Alata.". The count held at every checked anchor, but it remains a reconstruction from OCR and must be checked against the page scan. [USER: verify] 

**The name in the main text.** The OCR form of the Sanskrit name ("DandsLlu", with the long vowel lost) was searched, together with the forms Dandalu and Dandaluka, in the chapter files of `Sources/SS_Sushruta/text/` (sutra, chikitsa, uttara, kalpa, nidana, sharira). No line was found. The Samhita's chapter on tubers and bulbs (`SS_Sutra_ch46.txt`, lines 1922 to 1985) names other "aluka" bulbs (Pindaluka, Madhvaluka, Hastyaluka, Kasthaluka, Shankhaluka, Raktaluka) but not this one. The attestation therefore rests on the appendix line alone.

**No other text.** A search of the other source texts on disk (Dioscorides, Shennong, rGyud-bzhi, Ebers, Assyrian) for *Dioscorea alata* and the English names found nothing. Entries for other species of *Dioscorea* exist in the Codex; they are separate substances and are not used here.
