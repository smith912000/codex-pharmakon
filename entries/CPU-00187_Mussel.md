### Active Components

Mussels are bivalve molluscs: soft body tissue (protein, glycogen, some fat) inside a shell of calcium carbonate lined with nacre. They are filter feeders and concentrate whatever is in the water. The sources read give no composition.

### Scientific Correlation

Mussels are a food. The shell is mostly calcium carbonate. Bivalve shellfish can carry bacteria, viruses and algal toxins, and shellfish allergy is common. No clinical studies were retrieved for this entry. [USER: verify]

### Contraindications

Raw or undercooked mussels can carry dangerous bacteria, viruses and algal toxins (paralytic and diarrhetic shellfish poisoning), and shellfish allergy can be severe. Wild-harvested freshwater mussels are threatened in many places and may be protected; the status of *Hyriopsis cumingii* has not been checked here.
