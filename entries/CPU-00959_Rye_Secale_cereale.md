# Rye

## *Secale cereale* L. | Family: Poaceae (Gramineae)

**Tradition Tags:** CM, DMM *(see attestation note — the cereal grain vs. the ergot problem)*
**Energetic Classification:** As a temperate bread-grain, humoral streams treat rye as broadly cool/dry and nourishing but "coarser" than wheat — **inferred from the general grain-humoral scheme, not a firm species citation.**
**Training Phase:** Foundation — Signature Study

### Origin & Tradition

*Secale cereale* is an annual grass (Poaceae), a hardy cereal that tolerates cold, poor, acid soils better than wheat and so became the bread-grain of northern and eastern Europe. It grows tall (1–2 m) with a slender terminal spike; the harvested product is the caryopsis (grain). Rye is a food plant first and a "medicinal" only secondarily — and its most consequential entry in any materia medica is not the grain at all but the **fungus that infects it.**

**⚠️ The spine of this entry is ERGOT.** Rye is the cereal most susceptible to infection by the fungus ***Claviceps purpurea***. The fungus replaces developing grain with a hard, dark **sclerotium** ("ergot") packed with **ergot alkaloids** — chiefly **ergotamine** and **ergometrine (ergonovine)**, plus related lysergic-acid derivatives. **This must be stated plainly: the ergot alkaloids are the metabolites of a fungal CONTAMINANT growing on the grain — they are NOT constituents of rye itself.** A clean rye grain does not contain them; an infected ear does. This single distinction organises the whole entry.

That fungal contaminant is responsible for three otherwise-unrelated histories:
1. **Ergotism — "St Anthony's Fire."** Medieval epidemics from eating ergot-contaminated rye bread produced two syndromes: **gangrenous** (burning limbs, dry gangrene, loss of extremities from vasoconstriction) and **convulsive** (seizures, hallucinations, psychosis).
2. **Real obstetric and neurological drugs.** The same vasoconstrictor/uterotonic pharmacology, purified and dosed, gave medicine **ergometrine/methylergometrine** (uterine contraction — postpartum haemorrhage) and **ergotamine** (migraine abortive).
3. **The LSD story.** Lysergic acid from ergot was the scaffold from which Albert Hofmann synthesised LSD-25.

### Active Components

**In the grain itself (food):**
- **Dietary fibre**, notably **arabinoxylans** (the dominant soluble/insoluble fibre of rye), β-glucans, cellulose, fructans and resistant starch.
- Lignans, phenolic acids, vitamins and minerals typical of a whole grain.
- **Gluten proteins** (secalins) — relevant to coeliac disease.

**In the ergot sclerotium (fungal contaminant — NOT rye):**
- **Ergot alkaloids**: ergotamine, ergometrine (ergonovine), ergocristine and related ergopeptines; all lysergic-acid derivatives acting on serotonergic, adrenergic and dopaminergic receptors, producing potent vasoconstriction and uterotonic effects.

**As a separate commercial product:**
- **Rye-grass / rye pollen extract** ("Cernilton"/cernitin, from *Secale cereale* pollen) — a distinct preparation from either the grain or the ergot, with its own (modest) BPH trial history.

### Effect & Use Case in Kenetica

Kenetica files rye as a **"three-body" signature** whose entire teaching value is learning to keep three things apart:

1. **The grain** — a nourishing whole-grain fibre source with genuine, human-trial bowel and colonic-metabolism benefits.
2. **The fungal contaminant (ergot)** — a poison and a drug-precursor that is *on* the plant but not *of* it; source of both St Anthony's Fire and real uterotonic/migraine pharmaceuticals.
3. **The pollen extract** — a separate product with a separate, weaker evidence base for prostate symptoms.

Conflating these is the classic error, and the entry exists to prevent it. The honest cultivator's lesson is agronomic as much as pharmacological: **the medicine and the poison here are a storage-and-contamination problem** — ergot risk is managed by clean seed, crop rotation, sieving/cleaning grain and regulatory sclerotia limits, not by anything intrinsic to a healthy rye plant.

### Preparation & Dosing

| Form | Use | Notes / caveats |
|------|-----|------------------|
| Whole-grain rye bread / grain | Food; bowel regularity | RCT used ≥240 g/d whole-grain rye bread for 3 weeks (see below) — a dietary, not a "dose" |
| Ergot alkaloid drugs (ergometrine, ergotamine) | Prescription only | Micrograms–milligrams, clinician-administered; **never** from home-prepared ergot |
| Rye **pollen** extract (Cernilton/cernitin) | OTC phytotherapy for LUTS/BPH | Modest symptom benefit only; distinct from grain and ergot |
| Ergot-contaminated grain | **None — hazard** | Do not attempt any preparation; ergotism risk |

**No home ergot preparation is ever appropriate** — dosing crude ergot is uncontrollable and was historically lethal. Purified ergot drugs are hospital/prescription agents.

### Scientific Correlation

Grading each claim:

- **Ergot alkaloids are a fungal (Claviceps) contaminant, not a rye constituent: CONFIRMED / foundational** — this is the core honest framing.
- **Ergot alkaloids → ergotism and → real uterotonic/migraine drugs: WELL-ESTABLISHED** (historical + pharmacological review).
- **Ergometrine/methylergometrine for postpartum haemorrhage: ESTABLISHED** as second-line uterotonic; combination therapy (oxytocin + an ergot/other agent) outperforms oxytocin alone for prevention.
- **Ergotamine for migraine: ESTABLISHED but superseded/limited** — an effective vasoconstrictor abortive, but associated with medication-overuse headache and vascular risk; now largely displaced by triptans/gepants.
- **Rye grain relieves mild constipation / improves colonic metabolism: SUPPORTED by RCT** — whole-grain rye bread shortened transit time, increased defecation frequency, softened stool and raised faecal butyrate versus wheat bread and versus laxatives, without more GI side effects.
- **Rye pollen extract (Cernilton) for BPH: WEAK/MODEST** — Cochrane review found modest self-rated symptom and nocturia improvement but **no** change in flow rate, residual volume or prostate size, from short, small, methodologically limited trials.
- **Broader cardiometabolic/glycaemic benefit of whole-grain rye: PLAUSIBLE but not graded here** — a large literature exists; not individually verified this run, so left ungraded rather than asserted.

### Traditional Energetic Classification

No firm classical species-level energetic citation is established here. Within the general grain-humoral scheme rye is treated as a nourishing bread-grain, cooler/drier and "coarser" than wheat — presented as an **inference** from the grain group, not an attested classification. The medically decisive "hot/burning" signature historically linked to rye (St Anthony's *Fire*) is a description of **ergotism**, i.e. the fungal poison, and must not be read as an energetic property of the grain.

### Contraindications

- ⚠️ **Ergotism from contaminated grain** — never consume rye (or any cereal) with visible dark ergot sclerotia; gangrenous (vasoconstrictive limb ischaemia) and convulsive (seizure/psychosis) forms can be fatal. This is a food-safety hazard, managed by clean grain.
- ⚠️ **Ergot-alkaloid drugs (ergometrine, ergotamine): powerful vasoconstrictors.** Contraindicated in pregnancy for ergotamine (uterotonic/vasoconstrictor), and in uncontrolled hypertension, peripheral/coronary vascular disease, sepsis, and hepatic/renal impairment. **Serious interactions** with potent CYP3A4 inhibitors (macrolides, azole antifungals, some HIV protease inhibitors) — risk of severe ergotism; and additive vasoconstriction with triptans. Prescription-only.
- **Coeliac disease / gluten sensitivity** — rye contains gluten (secalins) and is **not** safe for coeliacs.
- **Rye pollen extract** is a distinct product; do not assume grain benefits transfer to it, or vice versa.
- General: do not self-prepare or dose any ergot-derived material.
