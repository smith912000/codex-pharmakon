# Munditika — Munditik^ (OCR), Sushruta Samhita, Bhishagratna Vol. III Appendix 3

## *Sphaeranthus hirtus, as Bhishagratna's Appendix 3 prints against the name (the OCR reads "Sapboerontheus Hirtus"); the Sanskrit name is garbled in the same way ("Munditik^"). The pairing rests on the position of the two entries in the appendix's parallel columns, not on any description of the plant; the genus Sphaeranthus is a small herb of the daisy family, and the name Bhishagratna prints is a variant of the one more often written S. indicus. Medium confidence for the genus, low for the species* | *(Volume I — Plant-Based)*

### Origin & Tradition

This is a single-text entry, and it rests on a name list, not on a passage of the treatise. The row was entered on 8 November 2026 from the residual key "Sapboerontheus Hirtus [SS]" with the locus "SS Vol. III Appendix 3 (line 32263; id line 32326)". The key was an OCR garble of the botanical name; this entry takes the Sanskrit name as the headword.

**Sushruta (SS).** Appendix No. 3 of Bhishagratna's Volume III is headed "Names of drugs (with Latin synonyms)" (`Sources/SS_Sushruta/text/Bhishagratna_Vol3_djvu.txt` line 31119). It prints the Sanskrit names in one column and the Latin names in a second, and the e-text separates the two columns. The Sanskrit name stands at line 32263, "Munditik^", between "Mulaki" (line 32261) and "Munja" (line 32265). The Latin column, at lines 32322 to 32330, runs in the same order: "Raphanus Sativus. Redish" (line 32325, matching Mulaki, which is the radish), then "Sapboerontheus Hirtus" (line 32326), then "Saccharum Munja" (line 32327, matching Munja). The pairing of Munditika with the Sphaeranthus entry follows from that order. Two anchors agree (the radish and the munja grass), and the OCR garbles in both columns keep the entry readable.

The name was searched for in the three volumes of Bhishagratna on disk and in the chapter files under variant spellings (Munditi, Mundik, Munditik). The only hit is the appendix line; the treatise's own text, as read, does not name a Munditika drug or give an action. A different appendix line, 32982, prints "Sphaeranthus Indicus" against another Sanskrit name; that is not this row, and whether the two are one plant is not decided here. [USER: verify]
