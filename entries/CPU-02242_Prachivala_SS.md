# Prachivala [SS] — Prachivala (Sushruta Samhita, Bhishagratna tr.)

## *A plant named once in the Surasadi group and once in a decoction for sprinkling a child; the translator himself marks the second as uncertain with "(?)", and no text on disk describes it; the identification is unresolved. Confidence: low* | *(Volume I — Plant-Based)*

### Origin & Tradition

**Named only by the Sushruta Samhita among the texts on disk.** This is a single-text entry. The row began on 13 October as an unverified placeholder, "Prachivala [SS]"; this entry replaces it. The text is Bhishagratna's English, `Sources/SS_Sushruta/text/Bhishagratna_Vol1_djvu.txt` and Vol. 3.

**Ayurveda (SS).** The group. Sutrasthana ch. 38, the Surasadi group (Vol. I lines 15460 to 15466; the name is split across a line break, "Prachi-vala", at lines 15464 to 15465): "Surasa, white Surasa, Fanjjhaka, Arjaka, Bhustrina, Sugandhaka, Sumukha, Kalamala, Kashamarda, Kshavaka, Kharpushpa, Vidanga, Katphala, Surasi, Nirgundi, Kulahala, Indurakarnika, Phanji, Prachivala, Kakamachi and Vishamushtika form the group known as the Surasadi Gana." Its effect (line 15470): "The group acts as a vermifuge and is an aseptic agent. It subdues the deranged Kapham and proves beneficial in catarrh, non-relish for food, asthma and cough."

The one other place. In the Uttaratantra chapter on the treatment of an attack by Sita-putana, a childhood affliction (ch. 34 as the e-text numbers it, Vol. III line 6896): "The decoction of Kapittha, Suvaha (Rasna), Vimbi, Vilva, Prachivala (?), Nandi (Vata) and Bhallataki should be used in sprinkling (the child's body)." The "(?)" is the translator's. No other occurrence of the name was found in the three volumes by a word search. [USER: verify against the Sanskrit.]
