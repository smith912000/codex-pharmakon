### Botanical Identity
Perennial herb 20–80cm; small oval aromatic leaves; pink-purple flowers; native to Mediterranean and Western Asia; many chemotypes with dramatically varying essential oil composition — critical pharmacological variable. The major chemotypes: carvacrol-type (Mediterranean wild, pharmacologically most active), thymol-type, sabinene hydrate-type (sweet marjoram), and mixed. High-carvacrol wild oregano (especially *O. compactum*, *O. vulgare* ssp. *hirtum*) is the pharmacologically potent form.

## Section 5 — Mechanisms of Action

### 5.1 Antimicrobial

**Carvacrol**: Disrupts bacterial cell membrane integrity (inserts into phospholipid bilayer → increases permeability → loss of cellular contents → cell death). Active against MRSA, *E. coli*, *Salmonella*, *Listeria*, *Candida*, *Aspergillus*, and multiple antibiotic-resistant strains. MIC values among the lowest of any natural compound against MRSA.

**Anti-biofilm**: Carvacrol disrupts quorum sensing (QS) → prevents biofilm formation — addresses one of the primary mechanisms of antibiotic resistance.

### 5.2 Anti-parasitic

**Carvacrol + thymol**: Active against intestinal parasites (*Giardia*, *Blastocystis*, *Leishmania*). Membrane disruption mechanism similar to bacterial activity. Multiple human studies confirm efficacy against intestinal protozoa.

### 5.3 Anti-inflammatory

**Carvacrol**: NF-κB inhibition; COX-2 inhibition; reduces TNF-α and IL-1β. **Rosmarinic acid**: 5-LOX inhibition; antioxidant. **β-Caryophyllene**: CB2 agonism.

## Section 6 — Clinical Evidence

**Intestinal parasites**: Phytother (2000): Oregano oil significantly reduces *Blastocystis hominis* and *Giardia* in human patients over 6-week treatment course.

**SIBO (Small Intestinal Bacterial Overgrowth)**: Pilot study: oregano oil significantly reduces SIBO symptoms and hydrogen breath test values.

**Antifungal (candidiasis)**: Multiple clinical reports; limited RCTs but strong mechanistic evidence.

**Oral antimicrobial**: Oregano-containing mouthwash significantly reduces oral pathogens.

## Section 7 — Phenomenological Record

**Mountain joy**: The Greek name *origanon* ("mountain joy") encodes an observation: oregano growing wild on Mediterranean hillsides is abundant, aromatic, and identifiable at a distance by fragrance. The "joy" may reflect the pleasure of finding such a useful plant growing freely — an antimicrobial, anti-parasitic herb available to every community near the Mediterranean coast. A plant that treats infection, prevents food spoilage (carvacrol is a food preservative), and relieves respiratory conditions would indeed produce joy in pre-antibiotic communities.

**The antimicrobial food preservative**: Oregano's widespread use in Mediterranean cuisine is not culturally arbitrary — carvacrol's antimicrobial properties prevent bacterial spoilage in olive oil preparations, meat marinades, and preserved foods. Traditional food culture embedded a powerful antimicrobial herb into daily diet, providing sustained low-dose antimicrobial exposure as a population-level prophylactic against food-borne infection.

## Section 8 — Practical Application

### 8.1 Preparation

| Form | Dose | Application |
|------|------|-------------|
| Oil of oregano (80%+ carvacrol) | 3–6 drops in water 3× daily | Acute antimicrobial; intestinal parasites |
| Capsule (standardised carvacrol) | 200–600mg carvacrol/day | Anti-parasitic; SIBO; antifungal |
| Dried leaf tea | 3–5g per cup | Respiratory; digestive; mild antimicrobial |
| Culinary fresh/dried | Liberally in food | Antimicrobial food preservation; chronic prevention |

### 8.2 Energetic Positioning

**TCM**: Acrid, warm. Channels: Lung (肺), Spleen (脾), Stomach (胃). Disperses cold-damp, warms middle, promotes Qi movement.

**Ayurvedic**: Pungent; hot. Reduces Kapha ↓↓, reduces Vata ↓, increases Pitta. *Krimighna, Dīpana*.

### 8.3 Contraindications

- **Pregnancy**: Emmenagogue — therapeutic doses avoid; culinary amounts safe
- **Iron absorption**: Phenols in oregano may reduce iron absorption — separate from iron supplements
- **Anticoagulants**: Mild antiplatelet — monitor with warfarin
- **Blood sugar**: Hypoglycaemic activity — monitor in T2DM with medication
- Dilute EO before ingestion (caustic undiluted)
