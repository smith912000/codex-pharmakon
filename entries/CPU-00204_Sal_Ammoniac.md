### Active Components

Sal ammoniac is ammonium chloride, a white, water-soluble salt that sublimes on heating and releases ammonia when mixed with a strong alkali. It occurs naturally near volcanic vents and burning coal seams and is also made industrially. The sources read give no composition.

### Scientific Correlation

Ammonium chloride is used as a flux in soldering, as a component of dry-cell batteries and, in medicine, as an expectorant and an acidifying agent. It irritates the stomach and can cause nausea and vomiting; large amounts cause acidosis. No clinical studies were retrieved for this entry. [USER: verify]

### Contraindications

Swallowed ammonium chloride irritates the gut and can cause vomiting and acid-base disturbance, and it is harmful in liver or kidney disease. Mixed with alkalis (lime, soda, wood ash) it releases ammonia gas, which burns the eyes and airways. The salt is not safe to take as a home remedy. Mineral medicines sold unregulated may be contaminated with other metals.
