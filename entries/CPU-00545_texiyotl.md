**Other traditions.** Dioscorides has chapters on stonecrops, which he groups with *aeizoon* (*Sempervivum*), and the translator's gloss for them includes other *Sedum* species. The genus is therefore shared in name only; the Dioscorides plants are other species, and they belong to that tradition. They are recorded in the batch report and are not placed here. The Ebers, Sushruta, rGyud-bzhi and Shennong name lists gave no locus.

**Overlap note.** No existing Volume I entry covers a *Sedum* under that genus name. I_202 (Rhodiola, *Rhodiola rosea*; tags TCM, FMT) concerns a plant that the Dioscorides translator also glosses as a *Sedum* (*S. rhodiola*), but it is a different plant. No entry was edited.
