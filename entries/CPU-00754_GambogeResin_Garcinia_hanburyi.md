### Origin & Tradition

Gamboge is not a leaf, a root, or a bark. It is a wound. When the trunk of *Garcinia hanburyi* is spirally incised, a latex bleeds into a hollow bamboo tube, hardens over weeks, and is knocked out as a cylindrical stick of brittle, orange-brown resin. Scrape it and the powder is a violent, saturated yellow. Grind it with water and it emulsifies into a paint. Swallow it and it may kill you. Few substances in the codex hold the aesthetic and the lethal so tightly in one body.

**Chinese Medicine (CM) — 藤黄, *Téng Huáng* ("Vine Yellow")**

Classical Chinese materia medica records *Teng Huang* as acrid, sour, cold, and — explicitly, unambiguously — **有大毒** (*yǒu dà dú*, "greatly toxic"). It is classed among the external-use and drastic-attack drugs rather than the tonics. Attested classical indications cluster around: carbuncles and furuncles (癰疽, *yōng jū*), scrofula (瘰癧, *luǒ lì* — chronic cervical lymphadenopathy), stubborn ringworm and tinea, parasitic worms, and — the indication that anchors this entry — **jaundice**. A 2025 review of the antitumour pharmacology of gamboge opens by noting the resin's use "for over 500 years… to treat scrofula, carbuncle, jaundice, furuncle, and other chronic and stubborn diseases" (Zhou et al., *Front Pharmacol*, 2025). The doctrinal logic is *gōng dú* — attacking toxin with toxin. Gamboge does not nourish. It besieges.

**Dravyaguna / Materia Medica of the Colonial Pharmacopoeias (DMM) — Gambogia, "Rattan Jaundice"**

Gamboge entered European pharmacy through the Siam and Cambodia trade and was carried in the London, Edinburgh, and United States pharmacopoeias for well over a century as **Gambogia** — a hydragogue cathartic and anthelmintic, almost never given alone. It appears as the drastic component of compound pills (the classical partner being aloes and, in some formulations, scammony and jalap), where the polypharmacy served to distribute and blunt an effect that in isolation was uncontrollable. The vernacular trade name "rattan jaundice" preserves the vine-like habit of the climbing trees from which the tubes were tapped and, simultaneously, the disease the yellow drug was thought to answer.

The same resin, in the same century, sat on the palette. Gamboge yellow is a documented artists' pigment used in illumination and in oil and watercolour practice — Rembrandt among the historical users. A physician's cathartic and a painter's lake were the same commodity, from the same tube, sold in the same warehouses. This is not a curiosity. It is the entire doctrinal engine of the entry.

**Sacred / Bodily Jurisprudence (SBJ)**

The gamboge tapping tradition sits inside Southeast Asian Buddhist monastic economies; the resin's saffron-adjacent colour and its use in dyeing cloth ties it, materially, to the visual identity of renunciation. The theological reading available here is a *purgation* reading: the substance that colours the robe is the substance that empties the body. **Doctrinal & Symbolic Layer**

The doctrine is a signature doctrine, and it is unusually legible: **yellow treats yellow.** A resin whose defining, market-defining property is the production of an intense bile-coloured stain was assigned to the disease whose defining sign is the intense bile-coloured staining of skin and sclera. The colour of the drug was read as a declaration of what the drug was for. This is the same inferential move as *Chelidonium* for the liver and turmeric for jaundice: sympathetic magic dressed as observation.

Map that onto real chemistry and the doctrine splits in two.

The **colour** is real and is chemically identifiable: the yellow of gamboge is carried by the caged prenylated xanthones, principally **gambogic acid** and **gambogenic acid**. These are the chromophores. They are also the cytotoxins. The pigment *is* the poison — there is no separation, no inert dye fraction hiding behind an active fraction. Rembrandt's yellow and the pharmacopoeia's drastic are the same molecule class doing the same electrophilic chemistry in two different substrates.

The **jaundice indication**, however, does not survive. Yellowness in the resin is a xanthone chromophore; yellowness in jaundice is bilirubin. The two share a hue and share nothing else — not biosynthesis, not structure, not metabolism. The signature is a false cognate. What gamboge actually did to a jaundiced patient was empty the bowel violently, which the pre-modern clinician read as the removal of the offending humour and which we would now read as fluid loss in someone already hepatically compromised. **The colour doctrine pointed at a real molecule and the wrong disease.**

The *carbuncle, scrofula, and stubborn nodule* indications are the ones that aged well. Those are the indications that survive translation into the modern literature — because "attacking a stubborn abnormal mass with a cytotoxin" is, structurally, chemotherapy.

### Active Components

- **Gambogic acid (GA)** — the principal caged xanthone; a Michael-acceptor electrophile. This is the compound carrying essentially all the modern interest.
- **Gambogenic acid (GNA)** — the closely related caged xanthone; the caged skeleton is described as "the fundamental pharmacophore responsible for its antitumor effects" (Huang et al., *Bioorg Chem*, 2024).
- **Resin acid / purgative fraction** — the crude resin's cathartic action is attributed to the resinous acid fraction acting as a severe intestinal irritant. [USER: verify — the specific purgative constituent is poorly characterised in the modern literature and older pharmacopoeial attributions should not be cited as though molecularly resolved.]
- **Gum fraction (~20–25%)** — water-soluble; enables the emulsion that makes it a workable paint.

### Effect & Use Case in Kenetica

**None. This substance is archived, not practised.**

Gamboge is retained in the codex as a *doctrinal specimen*: the cleanest available example of a signature doctrine that correctly identified a potent molecule and incorrectly identified its target. Its Kenetic use is epistemological. The practitioner studies it to learn what a false signature looks like from the inside — because from the inside, it looked persuasive for five hundred years, and the drug *did* do something dramatic every single time it was given, which is precisely how a wrong doctrine buys itself centuries of credibility.

Its second archival function is as a **traditional-to-modern lineage case**: the *scrofula and stubborn nodule* indication is currently being cashed out in oncology, while the *jaundice* indication is dead. Same plant. Same molecule. One indication survived contact with mechanism and one did not. That asymmetry is the lesson.

### Scientific Correlation

**Gambogic acid as an anticancer lead — Grade: MODERATE preclinical, WEAK clinical.**

According to PubMed, the caged-xanthone chemistry is genuinely active and mechanistically well-characterised, not folklore:

- Gambogic acid induces caspase-3/GSDME-dependent **pyroptosis** in ovarian cancer cells via a ROS/p53/mitochondrial pathway, with dose-dependent tumour inhibition in ID8 tumour-bearing mice and increased tumour-infiltrating CD3/CD4/CD8 lymphocytes at high dose — Zhang et al., *Biochem Pharmacol* 2024 [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bcp.2024.116695) (PMID 39643123).
- Chemical-proteomic work (ABPP, CETSA) identifies **peroxiredoxin-1 (PRDX1)** as a direct covalent binding target of gambogic acid, with GA-induced ROS, ER stress, and autophagy downstream, and PRDX1 overexpression rescuing the cell death — Wang et al., *Chin Med* 2026 [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13020-025-01144-8) (PMID 42237349). This paper states that GA "is currently in Phase II clinical trials for the treatment of cancer."
- Structure–activity work on 33 gambogenic acid–sulfoximine derivatives confirms the caged skeleton as the pharmacophore and identifies a lead (12k) acting via caspase-3/GSDME — Huang et al., *Bioorg Chem* 2024 [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bioorg.2024.107182) (PMID 38359707).
- Review of the antitumour ingredients and the 500-year traditional record — Zhou et al., *Front Pharmacol* 2025 [DOI](https://doi.org/10.3389/fphar.2025.1650560) (PMID 40900835).

**Honest grading.** Everything above is cell-line, xenograft, or mechanism work, plus a review's statement of Phase II status. I retrieved **no** published randomised controlled trial, and **no** published phase I/II efficacy or pharmacokinetic report, for gambogic acid in this run. The mechanistic case is strong; the human-outcome case is, on the evidence I can cite, **absent**. A covalent electrophile that generates ROS and hits an antioxidant enzyme is exactly the pharmacological profile that produces beautiful preclinical data and then fails on therapeutic index. Treat "in Phase II" as a statement about activity in a pipeline, not about efficacy in a patient.

**The jaundice indication — Grade: NULL, and actively deflated.** I retrieved no evidence of any kind — clinical, preclinical, or mechanistic — that gamboge or gambogic acid benefits jaundice, hyperbilirubinaemia, or any hepatobiliary disease. The doctrine that generated this indication was a colour match. Nothing in the retrieved literature supports it, and administering a severe cathartic to a patient with liver disease is affirmatively harmful. **This indication should be read as historically important and clinically abandoned.**

**The purgative action — Grade: STRONG historically, obsolete therapeutically.** That gamboge is a violent hydragogue cathartic is not in dispute; it is why it was in the pharmacopoeias and why it left them. I did not retrieve modern controlled data on the crude resin's purgative pharmacology, and none is needed: the drug is obsolete and no ethical trial exists.

### Cultivation Register

**Greek — Dioscorides (DMM).** *Garcinia hanburyi* is a Southeast Asian tree whose resin reached Western pharmacy only through the seventeenth-century Siam and Cambodia trade; it was wholly unknown to Dioscorides, who could not have described a commodity that arrived in Europe a millennium and a half after him. The material filed under "DMM" in this entry is in fact the record of the London, Edinburgh and United States pharmacopoeias — later European and pharmacopoeial practice, not the classical Greco-Roman corpus — where Gambogia was carried as a hydragogue cathartic hedged inside compound pills. Dioscorides assigns this substance nothing, because he never saw it, and the tradition carries no subtle-body doctrine to assign in any case. **(c) absent.**

**Unani — Avicenna (CM).** The same anachronism governs here: a maritime Southeast-Asian import that post-dates Avicenna by centuries does not appear in the Perso-Arabic materia medica, and the "CM" heading in the origin section is in fact attached to the Chinese *Teng Huang* material — a misassignment of the code rather than a genuine Unani attestation. The strongest Unani cultivation idea, the conservation of innate heat and radical moisture across a finite vital endowment, has nothing to act on because the tradition does not attest the substance at all. No status, no attestation. **(c) absent.**

**Chinese — Shennong / *Bencao* (SBJ).** Read the code by its registry meaning — SBJ is the Shennong classical *Bencao* stratum, not the "Sacred / Bodily Jurisprudence" gloss the body uses. Gamboge (藤黄, *Téng Huáng*, "vine yellow") is genuinely attested in Chinese materia medica, but as a later import worked for roughly five hundred years (Zhou et al., *Front Pharmacol*, 2025) — that is, at the post-classical stratum, not in the Han Shennong classic itself. The record is explicit and consistent: *Teng Huang* is acrid, sour, cold and 有大毒 (*yǒu dà dú*, greatly toxic), classed among the drastic external-use and toxin-attacking (*gōng dú* 攻毒) drugs rather than the tonics — the opposite pole from *yang sheng* 養生, the conservational nourishing of life. No *san pin* 三品 grade is recorded for this species in the sources retrieved, so its standing is a toxic-attack therapeutic one, not a cultivation status. **(b) framed** — and attested at the later (TCM) stratum, not the classical Shennong one the code names.

**Practitioner's note.** Nothing here is cultivated with; everything here is defended against. The one tradition that genuinely attests gamboge — Chinese materia medica — files it under its most emphatic toxicity marker and among the attack-drugs a cultivator is explicitly steered away from, while the two Western codes on the tag line never met the plant at all. A cultivator's whole relationship to this resin is the codex's: study the false "yellow-treats-yellow" signature, understand how a real cytotoxin bought five centuries of credibility by doing something dramatic every time, and administer none of it.

### Contraindications

⚠️ **GAMBOGE IS NOT A MEDICINE. IT IS A HISTORICAL POISON WITH A LIVING RESEARCH AFTERLIFE.**

- **Absolute:** all internal use, in any form, by any practitioner, for any indication. There is no Kenetic dose.
- **Severe purgative toxicity.** The crude resin produces violent catharsis — profuse watery stool, cramping, vomiting, and consequent dehydration and electrolyte derangement. Overdose is recorded in the historical pharmacopoeial and toxicological literature as fatal. The therapeutic-to-lethal window is narrow enough that the pharmacopoeias that carried it hedged it inside compound pills and then, eventually, dropped it.
- **Pregnancy, lactation:** absolutely contraindicated (drastic cathartic; uterine stimulation risk).
- **Paediatric:** absolutely contraindicated.
- **Hepatic or renal impairment, inflammatory bowel disease, bowel obstruction, dehydration, cardiac disease:** absolutely contraindicated — and note the vicious circularity that the historical *indication* (jaundice) is a modern *contraindication*.
- **Occupational/artistic:** gamboge pigment must not be handled as an inert colourant. Do not point the brush with the lips; do not sand or scrape dried paint without respiratory protection.
- **Research context:** gambogic acid's ROS-generating, covalent, PRDX1-binding mechanism implies off-target electrophilic reactivity across the proteome. It has no established human safety profile that I could retrieve.
