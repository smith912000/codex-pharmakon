### Active Components

Locusts are insects: a body of protein, fat and chitin in a hard outer cuticle. Some species concentrate plant toxins from what they eat. The sources read give no composition.

### Scientific Correlation

Locusts are eaten as food in some regions and are a protein source. They can trigger allergy, including in people with shellfish allergy, and wild-caught locusts that were sprayed with pesticides can be toxic. No medical use is established. ### Contraindications

Locusts can cause allergic reactions, and those gathered from sprayed fields can carry pesticide residues. Wild insects may also carry parasites and microbes and should not be eaten raw. Locust control uses poisons, and a swarm is not a safe source. The identification is uncertain, so nothing here can be applied to a particular insect. [USER: verify]
