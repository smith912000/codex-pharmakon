The sources read print no constituent. As a general fact, *Psoralea corylifolia* (family Fabaceae) seeds contain furocoumarins (psoralens), which make skin sensitive to light. Nothing is stated here about the drug as the Sushruta text had it. [USER: verify]

### Scientific Correlation

General, well-established facts only. *Psoralea corylifolia* seeds contain psoralen and related furocoumarins, compounds that sensitise skin to ultraviolet light and are a recognised cause of phototoxic reactions. No clinical studies were retrieved for this entry. [USER: verify]
