# I-192 · Dan Shen (Red Sage)
**Salvia miltiorrhiza** Bunge | Lamiaceae

---

## Section 1 — Nomenclature & Botanical Identity

**Accepted name:** *Salvia miltiorrhiza* Bunge (1833)  
**Common names:** Dan Shen, Chinese red sage, red sage, red-rooted sage, vermillion sage  
**Traditional names:**
- **Dan Shen (丹参):** "Red/Cinnabar Ginseng" — *dan* = red/cinnabar (the mineral), *shen* = ginseng/root; the brilliant red root color drove the naming; cinnabar = mercuric sulfide = color of transformation in Daoist alchemy
- Japanese: *Tanshen* (丹参); Korean: *Dansam* (단삼)
- Dioscoridean *Salvia* = broad genus; *S. miltiorrhiza* specifically is East Asian; *S. officinalis* (European sage) is the Mediterranean equivalent in Western herbalism

**Critical disambiguation — NOT culinary sage:**
- *Salvia officinalis* (European sage): aromatic culinary herb; thujone-containing; antimicrobial; different primary applications
- *Salvia miltiorrhiza* (Dan Shen): NO culinary use; dramatically different phytochemistry (abietane diterpenes absent in *S. officinalis*)
- Same genus (Salvia, ~960 species — third largest genus in angiosperms after Astragalus and Acacia); entirely different chemical profiles

**One ingredient — two compound classes:**

Dan Shen is unique in having two completely distinct, pharmacologically active compound classes:
1. **Water-soluble phenolic acids** (danshensu, salvianolic acids): anti-thrombotic, antioxidant — extracted in decoctions
2. **Lipid-soluble diterpenoid tanshinones** (tanshinone I, IIA, IIB, cryptotanshinone): anti-tumor, antimicrobial, cardiovascular — extracted in alcohol/oil

These two classes behave differently pharmacologically and require different extraction methods — creating formulation complexity.

**Botanical morphology:**
- Perennial herb to 80cm; square stems (Lamiaceae characteristic); opposite, pinnately compound leaves; blue-purple whorled flowers; brilliant red fibrous root system
- Root: 0.5–1.5cm diameter; 10–20cm long; external surface brick-red; interior yellow-white; easily broken; characteristic smell

**Part used:** Dried root and rhizome (*Radix et Rhizoma Salviae Miltiorrhizae*)

## Section 3 — Convergence Analysis

**"One Dan Shen equals four Si Wu Tang":**

A classical Chinese medical aphorism states *"一味丹参，功同四物"* — "one ingredient Dan Shen has the function of the four-ingredient Si Wu Tang (blood tonic formula)." This is a clinician's compression encoding that Dan Shen simultaneously: nourishes blood, activates blood, removes stasis, and calms the spirit — the four functions of the four-herb formula. Modern pharmacology supports this: Dan Shen's two compound classes address blood stasis (tanshinone antiplatelet, phenolic acid antithrombotic) and blood quality (phenolic acid antioxidant, cardiac protective) simultaneously.

**Tanshinone IIA — the anti-tumor diterpene:**

Tanshinone IIA has become one of the most intensively studied anti-tumor natural compounds in the world, with over 2,000 published research papers. Multiple mechanisms: topoisomerase II inhibition (same target as doxorubicin), NF-κB inhibition, cell cycle arrest (G1/S), ROS-mediated apoptosis in cancer cells, anti-angiogenic (VEGF inhibition). No current pharmaceutical registration for oncology use, but preclinical data is extensive.

**The cardiac convergence — ancient and modern:**

Traditional Chinese medicine classified Dan Shen as a treatment for *xiong bi* (胸痹) — "chest bi syndrome" = painful obstruction of the chest, corresponding to what we now recognize as angina pectoris and coronary artery disease. The modern pharmacology (coronary vasodilation via phenolic acids, anti-platelet via salvianolic acid B, NO upregulation) validates this specific traditional indication with molecular precision. This is one of the best documented cases of traditional indication → pharmacological validation in the Codex.

## Section 4 — Phytochemical Composition

**Class 1: Water-soluble Phenolic Acids**

- **Danshensu** (3,4-dihydroxyphenyl lactic acid): simplest active phenolic; anti-thrombotic; cardioprotective
- **Salvianolic acid A:** Rosmarinic acid + danshensu condensate; very potent antioxidant (Trolox equivalent ×10); thrombus inhibition
- **Salvianolic acid B (Lithospermic acid B / Magnesium lithospermate B):** PRIMARY water-soluble active; 3–12% dry weight; most potent antithrombotic phenolic; ACE inhibitor; nephroprotective
  - Magnesium chelate form (natural) = lithospermate B; bioavailable form
- **Rosmarinic acid:** Also present (shared with lemon balm, rosemary); COX-1/COX-2 inhibition

**Class 2: Lipid-soluble Diterpenoid Tanshinones**

- **Tanshinone IIA:** PRIMARY tanshinone (largest fraction); antitumor, antimicrobial, cardioprotective
- **Tanshinone I:** Second major; similar mechanisms; lower potency than IIA
- **Cryptotanshinone:** Unique — androgenic activity (5α-reductase inhibition); relevant for acne, androgen-dependent conditions; anti-tumor
- **Dihydrotanshinone I:** Antimicrobial; membrane disruption
- Total tanshinones: 0.2–1.0% dry weight

**Extraction partition:**
- Aqueous decoction: captures phenolic acids (danshensu, salvianolic acids, rosmarinic) → cardiovascular/antithrombotic focus
- Ethanol/lipid extract: captures tanshinones → antimicrobial/anti-tumor focus
- Most commercial standardized extracts: combine both fractions; specify both

## Section 5 — Mechanisms of Action

**1. Anti-thrombotic / Anti-platelet (Salvianolic Acid B, Danshensu — Primary)**
- Thromboxane A2 synthesis inhibition: ferulic acid pathway (shared with Dang Gui, Chuan Xiong)
- ADP-receptor (P2Y12) pathway inhibition: salvianolic acid B; similar to clopidogrel mechanism
- Thrombin inhibition: direct; salvianolic acid A + B
- Fibrinogen-binding inhibition: GP IIb/IIIa interaction reduction
- Net: multiple complementary anti-coagulant pathways; potent antithrombotic without formal anticoagulant status

**2. Coronary Vasodilation (Danshensu, Salvianolic Acids)**
- Endothelial NO synthase (eNOS) upregulation → increased NO → coronary vasodilation
- Adenosine A2A receptor agonism → coronary smooth muscle relaxation
- Ca²⁺ channel (VGCC) inhibition (mild; tanshinone contribution)
- Net: increased coronary blood flow; reduced vasospasm

**3. Anti-ischemic / Cardioprotective (All Fractions)**
- Mitochondrial protection: tanshinone IIA prevents mPTP opening under ischemia
- PI3K/Akt survival signaling: salvianolic acid B
- Antioxidant: salvianolic acid A (extremely potent); scavenges ROS during reperfusion
- Anti-apoptotic: Bcl-2 upregulation; caspase-3 inhibition
- Reduces infarct size in I/R models (well-replicated in animals)

**4. Anti-tumor (Tanshinone IIA — Extensive Preclinical)**
- Topoisomerase II inhibition: DNA strand break → apoptosis in rapidly dividing cells
- NF-κB inhibition → reduced pro-survival cytokines in tumor microenvironment
- Cell cycle arrest: G1/S and G2/M in cancer cell lines
- ROS-mediated selective apoptosis: cancer cells > normal cells
- Anti-angiogenic: VEGF and HIF-1α downregulation
- Multiple cancer types in vitro: breast (MDA-MB-231), colon, lung, prostate, leukemia
- Human evidence: pilot data only; no registered oncological use

**5. Androgens / Acne (Cryptotanshinone)**
- 5α-reductase type 2 inhibition → reduced DHT → anti-androgenic
- Relevant for: PCOS, androgenetic alopecia, acne vulgaris
- Evidence: preliminary; mechanistically sound; clinical trials ongoing

**6. Nephroprotective (Salvianolic Acid B)**
- Diabetic nephropathy animal models: reduces glomerular mesangial expansion
- TGF-β1 inhibition → reduced renal fibrosis
- ACE inhibitory activity: reduces glomerular hypertension
- Limited human data; clinical trials in progress

## Section 6 — Clinical Evidence

**Cardiovascular — Primary Evidence Domain:**

- *Danshen Dropping Pill* (复方丹参滴丸, compound Dan Shen formula): standardized preparation; completed Phase III FDA trial (NCT00797225) — one of the only traditional Chinese herbal preparations to complete US Phase III RCT process
  - Primary indication: stable angina; N=1,500+; primary endpoint not fully met; significant secondary endpoint improvements; represents landmark regulatory attempt
- Zhang WJ et al (2010) *Eur J Heart Fail*: Salvianolic acid B injection in heart failure; significant improvements in LVEF, BNP, 6-minute walk distance; N=180; P<0.01
- Chen YM et al (2012): Dan Shen + conventional therapy vs. conventional therapy alone for unstable angina; significant reduction in angina frequency and nitroglycerin consumption; multiple studies

**Diabetic Nephropathy:**
- Bi Y et al (2009): Dan Shen + ACE inhibitor vs. ACE inhibitor alone; reduced urinary albumin excretion; N=62; P<0.05

**Liver Fibrosis:**
- Ning B et al (2012): Salvianolic acid B in liver fibrosis; reduced fibrin markers in controlled study

**Regulatory:**
- China: Dan Shen preparations widely registered; IV forms for hospital cardiac use
- USA: Phase III completed (FDA); New Drug Application status complex
- EU: Not EMA approved; traditional use classification

## Section 7 — Phenomenological Record

**Red cinnabar — the alchemical root:**

The color of Dan Shen's roots — brick red, the color of cinnabar (mercuric sulfide, 丹砂) — embedded it in Daoist alchemical cosmology from the earliest records. Cinnabar was the color of the elixir of immortality; a blood-red root that "moves stagnant blood back to its proper circulation" resonated with alchemical transformation symbolism. The *Shennong Bencao Jing* classified it in the superior tier, meaning "taken over many years, it lightens the body and prolongs life." This is pharmacologically coherent: long-term antithrombotic + antioxidant + cardioprotective herb taken preventively as a cardiovascular tonic.

**FDA Phase III — a landmark attempt:**

The Danshen Dropping Pill (compound Dan Shen formula standardized tablet) becoming the first traditional Chinese herbal formula to undergo FDA Phase III trials represented a regulatory milestone. The trial (2006–2012) enrolled over 1,500 patients with stable angina at multiple international sites. The primary endpoint (exercise tolerance by treadmill) was not statistically met at the pre-specified threshold, but multiple secondary endpoints (angina frequency, nitroglycerin use, quality of life) showed significant improvements. The regulatory outcome was complex — not full approval, but generating the largest controlled dataset for any TCM formula in Western regulatory history.

**Heart failure and the phenolic acid injection:**

IV salvianolic acid B (Magnesium lithospermate B, Tianzhi Granules/Injection) has been used in Chinese hospital cardiology for heart failure. The intravenous formulation bypasses bioavailability challenges of oral tanshinone absorption, delivering salvianolic acid B directly. This creates a two-tier clinical reality: the oral herb/formula for prevention/mild disease; the IV pharmaceutical fraction for hospital-tier acute/severe disease.

**"Blood stasis" as the primary pathological concept:**

Chinese medicine's "blood stasis" (瘀血) concept — which underpins Dan Shen's primary application — has been described as the most important single concept in TCM cardiovascular medicine. Blood stasis produces: dark complexion, purple tongue (visible diagnosis), fixed boring pain, palpable masses, and irregular pulse — all clinical signs associated with cardiovascular, hepatic, and oncological pathology. Modern pharmacology has validated each element of the blood stasis syndrome: dark purple tongue = reduced microcirculation; fixed pain = ischemia; masses = fibrosis; irregular pulse = arrhythmia. Dan Shen's treatment of blood stasis addresses each measurable element.

## Section 8 — Practical Application

**Primary indications:**
- Coronary artery disease, angina pectoris (strongest evidence)
- Ischemic cardiovascular and cerebrovascular disease
- Heart failure (as adjunct)
- Diabetic nephropathy (as adjunct)
- Liver fibrosis (as adjunct)
- Menstrual irregularity with pain/blood stasis pattern
- PCOS / androgen-excess conditions (cryptotanshinone — emerging)

**Dosing:**
- **Standard dose:** 9–15g dried root as decoction daily
- **Dan Shen tincture (1:5, 60% ethanol):** 2–4 mL 3× daily; captures both fractions
- **Standardized extract:** 500–1,000mg/day; specify phenolic acid AND tanshinone content
- **Danshen Dropping Pill (Compound formula):** 10 pills 3× daily (standard Chinese patent dosing)
- **Duration:** 4–8 weeks acute; extended (months-years) for cardiovascular prevention

⚠️ **CRITICAL DRUG INTERACTION — WARFARIN:**

**Dan Shen + Warfarin = MAJOR CONTRAINDICATION in most clinical guidelines**
- Salvianolic acids + danshensu: multiple anticoagulant pathways → dramatically elevated INR
- Multiple case reports: INR > 10 with concurrent use; serious bleeding events
- **AVOID concurrent use or mandate very close INR monitoring**
- This is one of the two most important herbal-drug interactions in TCM (alongside SJW + HIV drugs, I-172)

**Other drug interactions:**
- **Digoxin:** Dan Shen reduces digoxin clearance → increased digoxin levels → toxicity risk; monitor digoxin levels
- **Antiplatelet drugs (aspirin, clopidogrel):** Additive anti-platelet; bleeding risk
- **CYP3A4 substrates:** Tanshinones weakly inhibit CYP3A4; monitor drug levels
- **Antihypertensives:** Vasodilatory effect → additive hypotension

⚠️ **CONTRAINDICATIONS:**
- **Warfarin / anticoagulant therapy:** MAJOR INTERACTION — avoid concurrent use
- **Hemorrhagic disease, active bleeding:** Anti-platelet/anticoagulant activity
- **Pregnancy:** Emmenagogue/blood-moving activity; AVOID
- **Blood deficiency without stasis (pale, thin, weak):** Blood-moving without nourishing action may deplete blood further; combine with Dang Gui and Shu Di Huang

**Standardization:**
- Chinese Pharmacopoeia 2020: minimum 0.2% tanshinone IIA + 3.0% salvianolic acid B (requiring both fractions)
- Full-spectrum extract preferred over isolated fraction for formulas
- Specify extraction solvent: aqueous extract for phenolics; ethanol for tanshinones

**Energetic tier:** ◉ Qi (circulatory/functional) — blood Qi activator at vascular/cardiac level; operates on circulation, flow, cardiac function; not constitutional but powerfully cardiovascular
